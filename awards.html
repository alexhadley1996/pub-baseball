How the ladders are built

LadderFormulaWho is eligible
MVP pointsOOTP WAR (batting + pitching) + 0.5 × WPA + 4 × (team winning % − .500)Eligible: 2.0 PA per team game, or 0.8 innings per team game.
Pitcher pointsOOTP pitching WAR + 0.5 × pitching WPA + 0.1 × (W − L) + 0.05 × savesEligible: 0.8 innings per team game, or relief appearances in 35% of team games.
Rookie pointsOOTP WAR (batting + pitching) + 0.5 × WPA, no team termEligible: a rookie (under 130 top-tier at-bats and under 50 top-tier innings before this season) with 1.5 PA or 0.5 innings per team game, or relief appearances in 30% of team games.

WAR and WPA are OOTP's own season figures from the export (players_career_*_stats.csv, 2002 rows). Points are in roughly WAR units: the team term moves a .600 club's player up 0.4 and a .400 club's down 0.4; a W-L of 5-1 is worth 0.4 on the pitcher ladder; 10 saves are worth 0.5. Award names come from leagues.csv. Players who changed teams count every stint in that league; the team shown is where he has played most. Blurbs quote only the numbers in the table.
