Two Teams Called the Red Sox

Boston Red Sox, 15-25 through 40 games

Here is the strangest thing about the Red Sox at the 40-game mark: where they play seems to matter more than who they play. 11-8 at home, 4-17 on the road.

40 games, by the numbers

  • 15-25 record
  • -46 run differential (157 scored, 203 allowed)
  • 4-8 in one-run games
  • 11-8 at home
  • 4-17 on the road
  • 4.54 / 4.68 starter / reliever ERA
  • 39 home runs
  • 16 stolen bases

Through 40 games they were 15-25, scoring 157 runs and allowing 203 (-46). With 41 games in the books as of May 13 they are 16-25, 9th in the East Division, 9 games back, and 19th of 20 in MLB. By the old Pythagorean arithmetic, that is a 15-win team so far. They are 4-8 in one-run games and 2-2 in extra innings.

The best bat has belonged to Pat Burrell, 25, who is hitting .298/.369/.504 with 5 home runs and 25 RBI in 157 plate appearances. Pedro Valdes leads the club with 6 home runs. As a team they are hitting .251/.319/.397, 16th of 20 in MLB by OPS through each club's first 40, with 39 home runs.

The rotation's anchor is Randy Johnson: 9 starts, 62.1 innings, 3.18 ERA. Pedro Astacio (3.33 ERA in 51.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 4.59 ERA, 19th of 20 in MLB; starters 4.54, relievers 4.68.

The surprise might be Pedro Valdes, who arrived without a 2001 top-tier line worth the name (under 150 plate appearances) and is hitting .274/.353/.500. On the other side of the ledger, Rich Aurilia is at .260/.298/.433, a long way from the .941 OPS he managed in 2001. In the rotation, Colby Lewis has a 8.08 ERA over 39.0 innings.

A couple of small, true things. When they score three runs or fewer they are 4-16; when they score six or more, 7-3. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

The best night was May 8, a 10-2 win over the Cardinals. The worst was Apr 19, a 15-1 loss to the Braves. Their best 10-game stretch went 6-4 (Apr 1 to Apr 10); their worst, 2-8 (Apr 19 to Apr 29).

MLB has no wild card and no consolation prize: division winners play for the title, the last-place club in each division goes down to the ABL. The Red Sox are 9 games behind the Marlins in the East Division. The floor is 2 games below them, where the Braves currently stand on relegation pace.

What comes next: 10 games starting May 14, 5 of them at home, against the Dodgers, the Marlins, the Blue Jays, the Mariners, opponents with a combined 212-198 record (.517). Neither a gift nor an ambush. The record matches the runs, so there is no regression bill coming and no hidden credit either. The next 122 games will mostly be about whether Pat Burrell keeps doing this. Forty games is when baseball stops being a rumor and starts being a record. The Red Sox have one now. It is theirs to keep or to fix.
