FBL · 2002-04-13 · By Marty Kessler, Diamond Daily

Felix Rodriguez Has a Bad Week

Five days after he blew a two-run lead in Memphis' 20-inning loss, Felix Rodriguez came in with the score tied in the ninth in Vancouver and gave up the winning hit to Todd Hollandsworth, who also homered. Mounties 6, Pharoahs 5.

VANCOUVER, B.C. — Felix Rodriguez came into the ninth inning on Saturday with the score tied, which is a better situation than the one he walked into five days earlier. It ended the same way.

Rodriguez got one out. Then Todd Hollandsworth, the hottest hitter in the FBL, singled to center to score the winning run, and the Vancouver Mounties beat the Memphis Pharoahs 6-5.

“I keep telling myself it’s baseball,” Rodriguez said. “It’s two weeks. It comes back. I have to believe that, because what else am I going to believe?”

Last Monday

Rodriguez’s week started in Memphis, against Inland Empire, in a game that lasted 20 innings. He came on in the ninth to protect a 2-0 lead and gave up both runs, and the Pharoahs went on to lose. It was the kind of night relievers say they forget about by the next morning. Most of them are lying.

Memphis manager Mike Palumbo said Saturday he had no hesitation about using him again. “He’s our guy,” Palumbo said. “He was our guy last week and he’s our guy this week. You don’t take a guy out of the job because of one bad night.”

He was asked whether two was different from one. “Ask me Monday,” he said.

The game

It started as a Mounties blowout. Erik Bedard, who’d thrown six shutout innings in that 20-inning game, didn’t have it this time. Jeff Reboulet, Vancouver’s 38-year-old second baseman, singled in two runs in the second inning, Bubba Crosby singled in another, and Hollandsworth homered in the third. It was 4-0.

Then Memphis took it from Dan Haren, Vancouver’s 21-year-old starter. Aaron Rowand singled in a run in the fourth and Geoff Jenkins added a sacrifice fly. In the fifth, Luis Castillo doubled in two to tie it, and Lou Merloni hit a sacrifice fly to put Memphis ahead 5-4.

“That’s the part I’m upset about,” Haren said. “We had four and I gave it back. I’ve got to be better than that.”

Jacobsen and Hollandsworth

Memphis led until the seventh, when Bucky Jacobsen, the Mounties’ big designated hitter, led off with a homer against Mike Fetters. Jimmy Serrano threw two scoreless innings for Vancouver, and the game went to the ninth tied.

Rodriguez came in. Crosby led off with a double, moved to third on a ground ball, and Edgar Martinez walked. Hollandsworth, who started the day hitting .475, lined the first pitch he saw into center.

He finished 2-for-5 with a homer and two RBI. He is still hitting .467.

“Felix is a good pitcher,” Hollandsworth said. “I’ve faced him before when he made me look silly. Today I was lucky. He threw me a fastball, and I’ve been seeing the fastball well.”

Vancouver is 6-6. Memphis has lost three straight and is 5-7.
