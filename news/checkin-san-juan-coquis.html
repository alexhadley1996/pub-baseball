40-Game Check-In · ABL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

Coquis at 40: It Is the Bullpen

San Juan Coquis, 20-20 through 40 games

The Coquis bullpen has a 7.35 ERA. That number explains more about a 20-20 record than any hitter or starter could.

Through 40 games they were 20-20, scoring 214 runs and allowing 245 (-31). As of the May 13 export they are 5th in the Eastern Conference, 3 games back, and 12th of 20 in the ABL. By the old Pythagorean arithmetic, that is a 18-win team so far. They are 5-4 in one-run games and 1-1 in extra innings.

What is driving it

J.D. Drew has been the Coquis hitter opponents least want to see: .264/.384/.507, 6 homers, 30 driven in. Marty Cordova leads the club with 9 home runs. Todd Walker has 9 steals (caught 5 times). As a team they are hitting .277/.354/.439, 16th of 20 in the ABL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 45 home runs.

Felix Diaz leads the starters with a 3.50 ERA over 43.2 innings (31 K, 11 BB). Mike Mussina (3.70 ERA in 65.2 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.78 ERA, 18th of 20 in the ABL; starters 5.08, relievers 7.35. Steve Karsay has 6 saves and 4 blown.

The surprise might be Alex Escobar, who arrived without a 2001 top-tier line worth the name (under 150 plate appearances) and is hitting .254/.345/.516. In the rotation, Sidney Ponson has a 7.87 ERA over 42.1 innings, after a 4.94 ERA in 2001.

A couple of small, true things. Todd Walker has walked 18 times and struck out 13, a ratio that belongs to a different, more patient century. They once scored 15 runs in a game (Apr 2, against the Angels); that one game is 7% of their season's runs.

The stakes

The ABL is the middle rung, so it is the only league here where you can fall and climb in the same breath: each conference winner gets a bye, second and third play in, both finalists of the American Championship are promoted to MLB, and the bottom three are relegated to the FBL. They sit 5th in the Eastern Conference, 3 games back of the Spiders. The drop is 2 games below them, where the Violets is the best of the clubs on relegation pace.

The best night was May 1, a 10-2 win over the Beavers. The worst was May 5, a 17-4 loss to the Spiders. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (Apr 17 to Apr 27); their worst, 2-8 (May 1 to May 11).

Looking ahead

The next stretch is 10 games (4 at home) against the Bees, the Orioles, the Padres, the Violets. Those clubs are 195-205 combined, a .487 winning percentage, so it will mostly be up to them. The longer-range question is whether the 2 or so wins they have banked beyond their run differential stay banked. History says to expect the runs to win that argument eventually; history is not always right in May. A quarter of a season tells you what a team is capable of. The other three quarters tell you what it is. For the Coquis, the interesting part starts now.
