Spirit at 40: Perfectly, Suspiciously Even

Albuquerque Spirit, 20-19 through 39 games

There is a special kind of season where nothing has gone very right and nothing has gone very wrong, and the Spirit are having it: 20-19, 196 runs scored, 201 allowed.

39 games, by the numbers

  • 20-19 record (39 games played)
  • -5 run differential (196 scored, 201 allowed)
  • 4-3 in one-run games
  • 10-9 at home
  • 10-10 on the road
  • 5.35 / 3.88 starter / reliever ERA
  • 44 home runs
  • 21 stolen bases

Through 39 games, all they had played when the export was taken on May 13, they were 20-19, with 196 runs scored and 201 allowed (-5). As of the May 13 export they are 5th in the West Group, 5½ games back, and 9th of 20 in the FBL. Runs scored and allowed like that typically buy about 19 wins in 39 games. They are 4-3 in one-run games and 1-0 in extra innings.

Darin Erstad has been the Spirit hitter opponents least want to see: .366/.419/.570, 2 homers, 13 driven in. Richie Sexson leads the club with 12 home runs. As a team they are hitting .265/.349/.437, 9th of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 44 home runs.

In the rotation, Victor Santos has been the steadiest: 3-2 with a 3.68 ERA, 37 strikeouts and 17 walks in 44.0 innings. Alan Benes (3.99 ERA in 38.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 4.79 ERA, 13th of 20 in the FBL; starters 5.35, relievers 3.88. Damaso Marte has 9 saves and 1 blown.

And it is not the line anyone would have written for him: his 2001 OPS was .691 in 711 plate appearances. The weakest regular bat has been Howie Clark, at .224/.285/.350 in 158 plate appearances. In the rotation, Tim Drew has a 8.21 ERA over 34.0 innings, after a 7.97 ERA in 2001.

Odds and ends: When they score three runs or fewer they are 1-12; when they score six or more, 16-0. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks. David Justice has walked 24 times and struck out 22, a ratio that belongs to a different, more patient century.

The best night was Apr 22, a 12-3 win over the Bats. The worst was May 2, an 11-0 loss to the Larks. Their best 10-game stretch went 8-2 (Apr 13 to Apr 22); their worst, 4-6 (Apr 1 to Apr 10).

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. They are 9th of 20 in the table, 1 game behind the Larks for the last ladder place.

The next stretch is 10 games (6 at home) against the Flying Squirrels, the Juicers, the Yard Goats, the Rebeldes. Those clubs are 188-209 combined, a .474 winning percentage, so it will mostly be up to them. One more thing to watch: Darin Erstad is on the milestone watch, closing on 100 home runs. In six weeks the Spirit have shown us who they might be. The next six will show us who they are willing to keep being.
