The Season So Far · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

Albuquerque's Arms Are Fine. Al Leiter Isn't One of Them, and Darin Erstad Is Hurt.

The Spirit's pitching got a D+ in March, with little behind Leiter and Damaso Marte. Leiter has a 6.26 ERA, the rest of the staff has held up, and the season changed when Erstad broke his foot.

Albuquerque Spirit · 20-19 · 196 runs scored, 201 allowed

Albuquerque's staff came into the year with a D+ and a simple diagnosis. It had two exciting arms, closer Damaso Marte and starter Al Leiter, and after them the falloff looked big and catastrophic. The lineup got a B, old but good.

Six weeks later, the diagnosis has flipped. Marte has been as good as promised, with nine saves, a 2.40 ERA, 20 strikeouts and four walks. Leiter, 36, is 3-6 with a 6.26 ERA and 27 walks in 54.2 innings, the most on the staff. The pitchers behind him have been anything but catastrophic. Victor Santos has a 3.68 ERA and has allowed one home run in 44 innings. Alan Benes is at 3.99. Ian Snell, 20, is 6-1 with a 2.45 ERA, mostly in relief with three spot starts, and Doug Brocail hasn't allowed a run in 10.1 innings.

The good weeks

Three weeks in, Albuquerque had won eight of 10, and its real identity was run prevention. Marte, Brocail, Snell and John Ennis had allowed one run combined in 35 innings. The best of it was a shocking home sweep of Vancouver from April 15 to 17, with the Mounties held to five runs in three games. The winning streak reached six, and the Spirit got to 14-10 on April 26 after a 14-inning win in Memphis and another win there the next night.

Leiter has had his good days, too. On May 8 he threw a five-hit shutout in Honolulu. But the team ERA is 4.79, 13th in the FBL, dragged down by Leiter and Tim Drew, who has an 8.21 ERA and has been knocked out in the third inning twice.

Erstad

The lineup turned out to need Darin Erstad more than anyone expected. He played his last game on April 26, hitting .366 with a 154 OPS+, a career year cut short by a fractured foot, the cruelest injury in the FBL. Albuquerque is 6-9 without him and has dropped four straight, three of them in Savannah.

The rest of the old, good lineup has held up. Richie Sexson has 12 homers and a 144 OPS+ despite missing time in early April, and on May 9 in Honolulu he hit two homers and drove in four. David Justice, 36, has eight home runs. Mark McLemore and Chad Kreuter, both 37, have been a little above average. Chris Donnels, a 36-year-old third baseman, has a 128 OPS+, 25 walks and a .401 on-base percentage. The problem is the outfield without Erstad. Howie Clark has a 64 OPS+ in center, and Quinton McCracken has a 77 in right. The Spirit are also last in the FBL in stolen bases, 21-for-34.

Where it stands

At 20-19 with a -5 run differential, Albuquerque is fifth in the West Group, 5.5 games behind Monterrey. The preseason projection of 80-71 is still within reach, and the site gives the Spirit a 31.0% chance at the playoffs. Richmond comes to town next, then three in Orlando and a visit from Hartford.
