The Season So Far · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

Albuquerque's Arms Are Fine. Al Leiter Isn't One of Them, and Darin Erstad Is Hurt.

Alexander gave the Spirit a D+ for pitching outside of Leiter and Damaso Marte. Leiter has a 6.26 ERA, the rest of the staff has held up, and the season changed when Erstad broke his foot.

Albuquerque Spirit · 20-19 · 196 runs scored, 201 allowed

Alexander's March grade for the Albuquerque staff was a D+, and he was specific about the reason. The Spirit had "exciting arms in Closer Damaso Marte and SP Al Leiter, but after that the fall off is big and catastrophic." The lineup got a B: "an old lineup, but a good one."

Six weeks later, about half of that holds up. Marte has nine saves, a 2.40 ERA, 20 strikeouts and four walks. Leiter, 36, is 3-6 with a 6.26 ERA and 27 walks in 54.2 innings, the most walks on the staff. The April 12 roundup noted him lasting 4.1 innings in Inland Empire. The pitchers after him in the rotation have been much better than "catastrophic." Victor Santos has a 3.68 ERA and has allowed one home run in 44 innings. Alan Benes is at 3.99. Ian Snell, 20, is 6-1 with a 2.45 ERA, mostly in relief, with three spot starts. Doug Brocail hasn't allowed a run in 10.1 innings.

The good week

At three weeks, Albuquerque had won eight of ten. Alexander's notes said "the real identity of the team is run prevention." He wrote that Marte, Brocail, Snell and John Ennis had "allowed just one run combined in 35 innings." The best of that stretch was a home sweep of Vancouver from April 15 to 17, with the Mounties held to five runs in three games. The same notes called it "a shocking sweep." The streak reached six wins, and the Spirit reached 14-10 by April 26 after a 14-inning win in Memphis and another win there the next night.

Leiter has had good days. On May 8 he threw a five-hit shutout in Honolulu. Still, the team ERA is 4.79, 13th in the FBL, mostly because of Leiter and Tim Drew (8.21). Drew has been knocked out in the third inning twice.

Erstad

The lineup needed Darin Erstad more than anyone expected. He played his last game on April 26, hitting .366/.419/.570 with a 154 OPS+. Alexander's injury report called his fractured foot "the cruelest FBL item... a career year in progress." Albuquerque is 6-9 without him and has dropped its last four, three of them in Savannah.

The rest of the "solid core of above average, experienced major leaguers" has held up. Richie Sexson has 12 home runs, a .614 slugging percentage and a 144 OPS+, even after missing time in early April. On May 9 in Honolulu he had two homers and four RBI. David Justice, 36, has eight home runs and a 112 OPS+. Mark McLemore and Chad Kreuter, both 37, are at 104 and 108. Chris Donnels, a 36-year-old third baseman not named in March, has a 128 OPS+, 25 walks and a .401 on-base percentage. The outfield without Erstad has been the problem. Howie Clark has a 64 OPS+ in center, and Quinton McCracken has a 77 in right. The Spirit are also last in the FBL in stolen bases, with 21 in 34 attempts.

Where it stands

At 20-19 with a -5 run differential, Albuquerque is fifth in the West Group and 5.5 games behind Monterrey. Alexander's 80-71 prediction is still within reach, and the site gives the Spirit a 31.0% chance at the playoffs. Richmond comes to town next, followed by three games in Orlando and a visit from Hartford.
