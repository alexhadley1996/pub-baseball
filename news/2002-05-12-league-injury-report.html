League Injury Report, Through May 12

Adam Everett's kneecap, Damian Moss's rotator cuff, Darin Erstad's .366 season interrupted, and two New Orleans outfielders down at once: the injuries reshaping all three leagues.

Six weeks in, the injury list has started making roster decisions for people. A broken kneecap in Dallas, a torn rotator cuff in Inland Empire and a shoulder in Baltimore have each removed a piece that a front office had planned around, and New Orleans has lost two outfielders at once. Here is everything that matters, through May 12.

The MLB headline is Adam Everett, out 9-10 months; he was hitting .194/.257/.299 for Dallas, so the loss is the glove, not the bat. Travis Hafner is the bigger offensive hole: .312/.394/.549 with seven homers for Washington before the fractured foot. Barry Bonds's sore knee is on the short list, which matters only because he is hitting .306/.466/.776 with 20 home runs.

Darin Erstad's fractured foot is the cruelest FBL item: he was hitting .366/.419/.570 for Albuquerque, a career year in progress. Frank Thomas (.321/.418/.661 in 67 plate appearances) and Damian Moss (6.65 ERA, 18 walks in 21.2 innings before the cuff) round out the significant list.

League Impact Summary

Biggest Season-Changers

  • Adam Everett (Dallas) and Damian Moss (Inland Empire) are effectively lost for the year — both teams must reshuffle defensive and pitching identities.
  • Noah Lowry’s shoulder injury is catastrophic for Baltimore’s rotation depth and likely forces an early trade or innings gamble.
  • Eric Young and Bernie Williams both going down for New Orleans strips speed, defense, and table-setting from an already thin offense.

Teams Hit Repeatedly

  • New Orleans (ABL): Multiple outfield injuries plus pitching attrition.
  • Honolulu (FBL): Middle infield + center field instability at once.
  • Philadelphia (MLB): Catching injuries stacking up early.

Quiet Winners

  • Miami (MLB) has largely avoided catastrophic injuries — one more reason their early success feels sustainable.
  • San Francisco (MLB) loses Ruben Sierra, but depth and defense cushion the blow.
  • Portland (ABL) dodges major injuries despite poor performance — trade leverage remains intact.

Early Warning Signs

  • Hamstring and shoulder injuries are clustering league-wide.
  • Several young pitchers (Lowry, Moss, Carrara) already showing durability red flags.
