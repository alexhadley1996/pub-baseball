40-Game Check-In · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

The Yard Goats Have a Sheffield Problem (the Good Kind)

Hartford Yard Goats, 22-17 through 39 games

Every 40-game sample produces a few lines that will not survive the summer, and a few that might. Gary Sheffield's .336/.412/.638, with 11 home runs in 177 plate appearances, is the line the Yard Goats would most like to keep.

Through 39 games, all they had played when the export was taken on May 13, they were 22-17, with 243 runs scored and 221 allowed (+22). As of the May 13 export they are 2nd in the East Group, 2 games back, and 5th of 20 in the FBL. By the old Pythagorean arithmetic, that is a 21-win team so far. They are 7-8 in one-run games and 1-1 in extra innings.

What is driving it

Gary Sheffield has 51 hits, 21 walks and 15 strikeouts in 177 trips; in 2001 he had a 1.000 OPS over 618 PA. Their most productive hitter is Randy Winn (CF, 27), at .346/.404/.603 with 7 HR. Gary Sheffield leads the club with 11 home runs. On the bases, Randy Winn is 10-for-14 stealing. As a team they are hitting .295/.356/.501, 2nd of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 53 home runs.

In the rotation, Chuck Finley has been the steadiest: 5-3 with a 2.92 ERA, 42 strikeouts and 12 walks in 61.2 innings. Masato Yoshii (4.30 ERA in 52.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.12 ERA, 17th of 20 in the FBL; starters 4.98, relievers 5.45. Closing has mostly meant Curt Leskanic: 9 saves, 3.78 ERA.

The surprise is Randy Winn. His 2001, by the export's count, was a .740 OPS over 480 plate appearances; this year it is 1.006. Players do not usually get 266 points better by accident, though they do sometimes get there by April. The weakest regular bat has been Santiago Perez, at .248/.292/.320 in 140 plate appearances. In the rotation, Doug Davis has a 7.38 ERA over 39.0 innings, after a 4.45 ERA in 2001.

One run. That is the margin that has defined the Hartford season: fifteen one-run games, a 7-8 record in them, and a 22-17 record overall that would look very different with a couple of those flipped.

The fine print is fun here. They once scored 16 runs in a game (May 9, against the Mounties); that one game is 7% of their season's runs. When they score three runs or fewer they are 1-9; when they score six or more, 18-4. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

The stakes

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. They are the 5th seed today: in the ladder, 2½ games out of an automatic promotion spot and 1½ games clear of the first team outside.

The best night was May 9, a 16-4 win over the Mounties. The worst was May 11, a 17-3 loss to the Twisters. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (Apr 16 to Apr 26); their worst, 2-8 (Apr 6 to Apr 16).

Looking ahead

What comes next: 10 games starting May 14, 4 of them at home, against the Bats, the Pharoahs, the Spirit, the Larks, opponents with a combined 210-183 record (.534). That is a real test. One more thing to watch: Gary Sheffield is on the milestone watch, closing on 1,000 walks.
