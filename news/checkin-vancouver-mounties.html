The Season So Far · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

Vancouver Got an F+ for Pitching. Jamie Moyer Didn't Notice.

The Mounties were ranked 18th, with Dan Haren on the way and nothing else on the mound. Moyer has been the steadiest starter, the bullpen has blown two saves, and Vancouver is 17-6 at home.

Vancouver Mounties · 23-17 · 218 runs scored, 187 allowed

Vancouver's preseason scouting report was short. The offense, graded C+, came down to three names: Edgar Martinez, Todd Hollandsworth and Mark Bellhorn. The pitching got an F+, with Dan Haren in the wings and nothing else. The consensus ranked the Mounties 18th of 20, and the projections had them at 75-76.

Vancouver is 23-17, third in the West Group and three games behind Monterrey, with a +31 run differential, the fourth-best record in the FBL and a 70.0% chance at the playoffs. The site projects about 81 wins.

Nothing else

That "nothing else" included Jamie Moyer. The 39-year-old threw eight shutout innings in Inland Empire on Opening Day and eight more against Oklahoma City on April 6 in a 13-0 win. By April 12 he was 3-0 and had allowed two runs in 24 innings. He's at 3.54 now, with 12 walks in 56 innings. Dicky Gonzalez, 23, is 5-2 with a 4.29 ERA and two eight-inning starts in which he allowed one run.

Haren has had the strangest season on the staff. His first start, on April 2, went seven innings with two earned runs, the second on a late homer in a 3-2 loss. He's 2-4 with a 6.92 ERA, and yet his FIP is 3.45, built on 34 strikeouts, 11 walks and two homers in 40.1 innings. Two early knockouts, at Monterrey and in Hartford, account for a lot of that ERA. The team ERA is 4.54, seventh in the FBL. The bullpen is at 3.68 and has blown two saves all season, and Jose Mesa is 11-for-11.

Three names, and a fourth

All three hitters have delivered. Bellhorn has 11 homers, 41 strikeouts and a 156 OPS+. Hollandsworth, one of the league's best all-around players three weeks in, is hitting .342 and doubled four times on Opening Day. Martinez, 39, has a .414 on-base percentage and a 143 OPS+. The fourth name is Bucky Jacobsen, a 26-year-old first baseman with 11 home runs and a team-high 36 RBI, seven of them in Friday's 10-7 win in Hartford.

After those four, the lineup drops off. Center fielder Bubba Crosby has a 66 OPS+, second baseman Jeff Reboulet, 38, is at 69, and Marcus Thames is at 62 without a homer. Vancouver is also 18th in the FBL in steals.

Home

The Mounties are 17-6 at home and 6-11 on the road, and as one of the league's energy-driven teams, they seem to feed off the home crowd. At home they've swept Memphis, Eugene and Orlando and taken their last series from San Antonio. On the road they were swept in Albuquerque in mid-April, scoring five runs in three games. They're 7-3 in their last 10. Next come three in Alaska, then Sacramento visits for a series between the West's second- and third-place teams before a trip to Hoboken.
