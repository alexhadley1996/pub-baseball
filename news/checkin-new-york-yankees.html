40-Game Check-In · MLB · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

The Yankees Have a Bonds Problem (the Good Kind)

New York Yankees, 21-19 through 40 games

Every 40-game sample produces a few lines that will not survive the summer, and a few that might. Barry Bonds's .305/.462/.779, with 20 home runs in 171 plate appearances, is the line the Yankees would most like to keep.

Barry Bonds has 40 hits, 35 walks and 23 strikeouts in 171 trips; in 2001 he had a 1.379 OPS over 664 PA. Torii Hunter has been the Yankees hitter opponents least want to see: .286/.360/.481, 5 homers, 17 driven in. Barry Bonds leads the club with 20 home runs. As a team they are hitting .271/.339/.431, 4th of 20 in MLB by OPS through each club's first 40, with 49 home runs.

In the rotation, Greg Maddux has been the steadiest: 6-1 with a 2.48 ERA, 43 strikeouts and 13 walks in 69.0 innings. Chuck Smith (3.50 ERA in 54.0 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 4.01 ERA, 11th of 20 in MLB; starters 3.99, relievers 4.05. Closing has mostly meant Mike DeJean: 9 saves, 2.04 ERA.

Through 40 games they were 21-19, scoring 192 runs and allowing 182 (+10). With 41 games in the books as of May 13 they are 22-19, 4th in the East Division, 3 games back, and 7th of 20 in MLB. By the old Pythagorean arithmetic, that is a 21-win team so far. They are 6-8 in one-run games and 3-4 in extra innings.

The surprise might be Lenny Harris, who arrived without a 2001 top-tier line worth the name (under 150 plate appearances) and is hitting .319/.362/.471. The weakest regular bat has been Fernando Seguignol, at .203/.263/.353 in 167 plate appearances. In the rotation, Ariel Prieto has a 7.22 ERA over 33.2 innings.

The fine print is fun here. Barry Bonds has walked 35 times and struck out 23, a ratio that belongs to a different, more patient century. They once scored 14 runs in a game (Apr 29, against the Phillies); that one game is 7% of their season's runs.

The best night was Apr 27, a 12-3 win over the Phillies. The worst was May 1, a 15-1 loss to the Cubs. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (Apr 25 to May 5); their worst, 3-7 (Apr 7 to Apr 17).

Nine innings have not been enough for the Yankees, seven times. They are 3-4 in those games and 21-19 overall.

MLB has no wild card and no consolation prize: division winners play for the title, the last-place club in each division goes down to the ABL. The Yankees are 3 games behind the Marlins in the East Division. The floor is 8 games below them, where the Braves currently stand on relegation pace.

What comes next: 10 games starting May 14, 5 of them at home, against the Griffins, the Gothams, the Wolverines, the Marlins, opponents with a combined 227-183 record (.554). That is a real test. One more thing to watch: Barry Bonds is on the milestone watch, closing on 600 home runs.

A quarter of a season tells you what a team is capable of. The other three quarters tell you what it is. For the Yankees, the interesting part starts now.
