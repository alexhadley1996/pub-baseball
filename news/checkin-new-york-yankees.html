The Season So Far · MLB · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

Barry Bonds Has 20 Home Runs and the Yankees Are Third in Line

Bonds hit 10 homers in 50 May plate appearances and has a 230 OPS+. The Yankees, Alexander's preseason No. 1, are 22-19 and three games out, with the whole East race on their next nine dates.

New York Yankees · 22-19 · 201 runs scored, 184 allowed

Barry Bonds has come to the plate 50 times in May and hit 10 home runs. For the season he is at .306/.466/.776 with 20 homers, 42 RBI, 37 walks, an OPS+ of 230 and 3.2 WAR, as much as Greg Maddux and Chuck Smith combined. Alexander's 40-game notes put it simply: "There's no telling what Bonds will be able to do this year in Yankee Stadium with 20 HR in his team's first 40 games." The odd part is that the Yankees, with this Bonds, are 22-19 and fourth in the East, three games behind Miami.

"Bonds and vibes"

Alexander ranked the Yankees first in MLB in March with a scouting line that has held up all spring. He described the offense as "Bonds and vibes," good defenders around him, and a question: "opposing teams may simply avoid Bonds and dare everyone else." Opponents have walked Bonds 37 times. Everyone else has been mostly fine. Lenny Harris, 37, is hitting .319/.362/.468. Torii Hunter is at .287/.367/.484. Both are the "great (though perhaps beneficiaries of high BABIPs)" bats Alexander called out. The weak spots are in right field, where Fernando Seguignol has a .262 OBP and -0.8 WAR, and at second base and DH. Alexander found those choices the most puzzling on the club. Carlos Baerga, back in MLB this year, is hitting .328/.386/.531 in 70 plate appearances. Joe Torre has stayed with Mark Grudzielanek at second and kept Adam Kennedy at DH, where Kennedy has 196 plate appearances, the most on the team, and no home runs. "It seems obvious," Alexander wrote of the fix, "but he seems to want to go with the young man."

The best rotation in the league, give or take

The pitching grade in March was an A: "the best rotation in the league, led by Maddux." Greg Maddux is 6-1 with a 2.48 ERA, two complete games and a shutout of Washington on April 5. He has also thrown eight shutout innings to beat Toronto 1-0. Chuck Smith has 51 strikeouts and a 2.92 FIP, and Alexander called him "great." Kris Benson is 1-5 with a 3.56 FIP and, in Alexander's word, "unlucky." He threw a complete game in Dallas on May 13 for his first win. The fifth spot is the problem. Ariel Prieto has a 7.22 ERA, and Alexander expects reinforcements for him and for Jack Cressend, whose 14.62 ERA in eight innings has dragged down an otherwise solid bullpen.

A season of very long games

The Yankees have played seven extra-inning games and gone 3-4. In April they lost a 15-inning game in Atlanta 3-2, then won a 14-inning game there two days later. On April 15 in Phoenix they blew an eight-run lead and lost 9-8 in 12. The low point was April 7 to 17, a 3-7 stretch that included a three-game sweep by the Seals at the Stadium. Alexander called April "intensely mediocre," and they finished the month 14-15.

Then the Bonds month began, and it has had a sense of humor. On April 29 in Philadelphia the Yankees trailed by seven runs and won 14-12. Bonds went 2-for-3 with two homers, four RBI and three walks. On May 1 they lost 15-1 at Wrigley Field. The next day they were down by five, and Bonds hit two more home runs and drove in five in an 8-7 win in 10 innings. They are 8-4 in May.

From here

MLB sends only its division winners to the World Series, a best-of-nine, and relegates the last-place club in each division. The Yankees' March prediction was 82-69, third in the East. The site's simulations, which weight a 41-game record and a modest +17 run differential, give them an 11.4% chance at the division. Alexander is more confident: "With Bonds rolling and the rotation clicking, look for the Yankees to take over the East from Detroit in the near future. ... My prediction: East Division champions." The schedule is about to decide this. After two games in Dallas, the next seven are three at home against Brooklyn, three in Detroit and one against Miami: the three clubs ahead of them, in a row. If Alexander's call is going to come true, this is where it starts.
