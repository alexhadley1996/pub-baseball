ABL · 2002-04-18 · By Ken Albrecht, The Baseball Courier

New Orleans Doesn't Do Short Games

New Orleans led Pittsburgh 5-1, trailed 8-5, tied it in the ninth on Ruben Rivera's two-run double off B.J. Ryan, and won 9-8 in the 10th on Raul Mondesi's fly ball. It was the Athletics' third extra-inning game in a week.

NEW ORLEANS — For the third time in a week, the New Orleans Athletics went to extra innings. For the second time, they won.

On Thursday night they beat the Pittsburgh Pirates 9-8 in 10. Last Friday they beat the New York Knights 12-11 in 10. On Tuesday, in between, they lost in Minnesota 8-7 in 13. Their manager, Darius White, says he hasn’t slept more than five hours a night since Easter.

“I’m thinking of telling the guys we’re not allowed to tie anymore,” White said. “Just win in nine or lose in nine. My heart can’t take this.”

The early lead

It started well. Bernie Williams singled in a run in the first, Raul Mondesi tripled in another, and Nick Swisher grounded in a third. In the third Swisher tripled in a run and Ruben Rivera singled him home, and New Orleans led 5-1 behind Casey Fossum.

Fossum didn’t hold it. In the fifth the Pirates got a run-scoring single from Shannon Stewart and a sacrifice fly from Aramis Ramirez, and Nomar Garciaparra homered with two outs to tie it 5-5. Nomar now has 23 RBI in 17 games.

“He’s the best shortstop in this league,” White said. “Maybe any league. We made a mistake and he made us pay. That’s what great players do.”

Dave Berg doubled in the go-ahead run in the sixth, Dan Wilson singled in another, and Stewart added a sacrifice fly in the eighth. 8-5.

Jose Reyes

A word about the other Pittsburgh shortstop, because he deserves one. Jose Reyes is 18 years old and is supposed to be in Class A. Instead he’s the Pirates’ designated hitter, and on Thursday he had three more hits and stole two more bases. He’s hitting .390.

“I keep waiting for him to look 18,” Pirates manager Lloyd McClendon said. “Hasn’t happened yet.”

The comeback

New Orleans cut it to 8-6 on Eric Young’s two-out single in the eighth. In the ninth, B.J. Ryan, Pittsburgh’s closer, came on with four saves and an ERA of 0.00. He struck out Williams. Then Mondesi singled, Swisher was hit by a pitch, and Rivera doubled into the corner in left to score them both. 8-8.

“That’s my guy,” White said. “Ruben’s been in the middle of everything. Last week, Tuesday, tonight. Some guys like the big moment. He likes it.”

The 10th

Danys Baez and Chad Fox kept the Pirates quiet in the 10th. In the bottom half Ryan was still pitching. Tim Bogar struck out but reached when the third strike got away. Young singled, Brian Roberts flied out, Williams walked to load the bases, and Mondesi lifted a fly ball to right. Bogar came home, and the game was over.

Mondesi finished with three hits and three runs scored. Rivera drove in three. New Orleans is 9-8.

The Pirates are 10-7. McClendon wasn’t much interested in the standings Thursday night.

“We had 8-5 in the ninth,” he said. “You can’t lose that game. We lost that game.”
