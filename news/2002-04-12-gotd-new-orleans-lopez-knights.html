ABL · 2002-04-12 · By Paul Ricci, Sporting Weekly

The Knights Lose One They Had

The New York Knights led New Orleans 8-1 in the seventh. New Orleans scored seven in the seventh, one in the eighth, two more in the ninth after the Knights went back ahead, and won 12-11 in the 10th on Fabian Lopez's two-out single.

NEW ORLEANS — Bobby Valentine has spent two weeks trying to convince people that his New York Knights are better than they looked last October, when the Yankees swept them in four games by a combined 39-5. On Friday night, for six innings, the Knights made the argument for him.

They led the New Orleans Athletics 8-1 going to the bottom of the seventh. They lost 12-11 in 10 innings.

“I’m not going to say anything I’ll regret,” Valentine said. He then went on to say a number of things, none of them printable, most of them about the seventh inning.

Six good innings

Kerry Wood started for New Orleans and struck out eight, but he walked two and the Knights made him pay early. Lance Berkman grounded in a run in the first, Eric Chavez added a sacrifice fly, and Kelly Stinnett singled in two more with two outs. Gene Kingsale singled in runs in the third and the fifth. Berkman, hitting .432 with no homers, hit his first of the season in the sixth, a two-run shot off Brian Falkenborg.

Tim Hudson, meanwhile, gave up a run in six innings. It was 8-1.

The seventh

Hudson started the seventh and loaded the bases without getting an out. Valentine left him in. Eric Young walked to force in a run. Brian Roberts doubled to clear the bases, and it was 8-5. Hudson was done.

J.C. Romero came on and did not stop it. Raul Mondesi singled in a run. Ruben Rivera doubled in two, and the game was tied.

“I went to Hudson because he’d been great,” Valentine said. “If you want to second-guess me, go ahead. I’ve been doing it myself for an hour.”

In the eighth, Roberts hit a sacrifice fly off Brian Boehringer, and New Orleans led 9-8.

The ninth

The Knights, to their credit, didn’t fold. In the ninth Kingsale doubled in two off Ray King to put New York back ahead, and Carlos Guillen singled in another. 11-9. Kingsale finished 4-for-5 with four RBI.

Scott Stewart came on to finish it. With two outs and the bases loaded, Young singled in two runs, and it was 11-11.

Lopez

Dave Coggin hit a batter and walked another in the 10th, then got a ground ball to end the inning. In the bottom half Joe Nathan, who came in 2-0 with a 1.17 ERA, gave up a one-out single to Mondesi, walked Nick Swisher and struck out Rivera. Fabian Lopez, a backup infielder who’d come into the game late, singled into left. Mondesi scored from second.

“I’ve been waiting two weeks to play,” Lopez said. “I didn’t need much.”

Roberts, the new man in town, finished with three hits and four RBI. Eric Young had three hits and three RBI. New Orleans is 5-6.

The Knights are 4-7. They have a lot of time to get better. They don’t have a lot of time to keep losing games like this one.
