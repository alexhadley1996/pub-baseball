Roundup · 2002-04-06 · By Alexander Hadley · Alexander's league doc, daily roundups

Roundup, April 6: Monterrey 10, Sacramento 9

3 tiers (FBL, ABL, MLB), 25 games noted.

FBL Daily highlights

  • Monterrey 10, Sacramento 9: Sultanas rallied from 8–0 down by the 3rd; Alex Ochoa went 4-for-5 with 2 HR, including a tying 3-run HR in the 9th off Octavio Dotel. Box: SACR 9, MONT 10 (10)
  • Alaska 3, Honolulu 0: Matt Morris masterpiece: 1 hit, 3 BB, 13 K. Box: ALSK 3, HONO 0
  • Vancouver 13, Oklahoma City 0: Jamie Moyer threw 8 shutout; Hollandsworth/Bellhorn/Martínez kept the line moving. Box: OKC 0, VANC 13
  • San Antonio 4, Savannah 3: Josh Beckett outdueled Barry Zito; Julio Lugo 2-run HR; Jason Conti 2-run double. Box: SARe 4, SaSa 3
  • Santo Domingo edged Orlando late: rally ended on a sacrifice fly; Javier Vázquez went 8.1 dominant. Box: ORLJ 1, SDG 2
  • Carolina HR barrage: Ken Griffey Jr. homered; Pablo Sánchez hit his first big-league HR and a triple. Box: ALBQ 5, CDT 8
  • Hoboken shut out Eugene: Roy Halladay 8 shutout, 8 K, 1 BB. Box: EUGL 0, HOBK 2
  • Richmond handed Hartford its first loss: Greg Colbrunn went 3-for-3, 2 RBI, double. Box: HYG 3, RiFS 4
  • Charlotte: Curt Schilling struck out 12; only real damage a 2-run HR by Greg Norton (now 5 HR in 6 games). Box: MePh 3, ChKn 4

ABL Daily highlights

  • Tampa Bay rally attempt vs Southside fell short: Southside won in extras with solo HR from Nick Johnson and Larry Sutton. Box: SBS 6, TBR 5 (11)
  • Pittsburgh 13, Austin 0: Pirates to 5–1; Joel Piñeiro 6.2 shutout; offense walked 7 times and piled up 17 hits—bottom of the order went berserk. Box: AUS 0, PIT 13
  • Cleveland 5, Salt Lake 2: ace Jason Schmidt went 8.1; only blemish two solo HR. Box: SLB 2, CLE 5
  • New Orleans comeback: scored six in the 7th off Mike Mussina and Roberto Hernández to steal a 6–5 win; bullpen (especially Danny Báez) bailed out Kerry Wood’s shaky command. Box: SJC 5, NOLA 6
  • Mexico City 7, Nashville 5: Jim Thome and Adam Dunn drove in three and scored three. Box: NASH 5, MEXC 7
  • KC 5, Las Vegas 2: Larry Walker RBI double and a 2-run HR. Box: LVG 2, KCM 5
  • Orioles dramatic win: late rally vs Portland closer Troy Percival; Roy Oswalt strong but nearly wasted. Box: BAL 4, PoBe 2
  • Knights 4, Anaheim 2 (10): clutch extra-inning hitting off Luis Ayala. Box: NYK 4, LAA 2 (10)
  • Padres 8, Reds 4: Phil Nevin hit two HR, including a huge 2-run shot in the 7th. Box: SDP 8, CIN 4

MLB Daily highlights

  • Marlins 3, Seals 2: Miami got to Jake Peavy in the 7th for three runs; bottom of the order delivered—Brian Schneider and Cristian Guzmán key hits. Bullpen (including Danny Graves and Kazuhiro Sasaki) closed again. Box: SFS 2, MIA 3
  • Wolverines 4, D-backs 0: Denny Neagle threw 7 shutout. Box: ARI 0, DET 4
  • Red Sox walk off A’s: Oakland starter Jeff D’Amico went 8, 0 BB, but closer Jim Mann failed to record an out; Boston won on a Rich Aurilia walk-off single. Box: OAK 2, BOS 3
  • Brooklyn blew up St. Louis pitching: 22 hits; rookie Zack Greinke got tagged for 6 runs in 4 IP; Ted Lilly went 8, 1 ER. Standouts: Bobby Higginson 4-for-6, Endy Chávez 3-for-5, Jack Wilson 4-for-6. Box: BrGo 10, STL 1
  • Rockies 3, Cubs 1: Brian Moehler escaped 7.1 with one run; Oliver Pérez closed it down. Box: COL 3, CHC 1
  • Yankees 5, Cherry Blossoms 3: Fernando Seguignol RBI double and a 2-run HR. Box: WCB 3, NYY 5
  • Phillies 7, Astros 3: Miguel Cabrera hit a solo HR in the 7th; Houston couldn’t solve Esteban Loaiza. Box: HOU 3, PHI 7
