40-Game Check-In · ABL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

The Golden Eagles Have a Thome Problem (the Good Kind)

Mexico City Golden Eagles, 18-22 through 40 games

The cleanest way to describe the first 40 games of Jim Thome is with plain counting: 49 hits, 10 home runs and 32 walks in 175 plate appearances. The Golden Eagles are 18-22, and he is a large part of the numerator.

The ABL is the middle rung, so it is the only league here where you can fall and climb in the same breath: each conference winner gets a bye, second and third play in, both finalists of the American Championship are promoted to MLB, and the bottom three are relegated to the FBL. They sit 10th in the Western Conference, 8 games back of the Black Sox. The less fun line: the site has them on relegation pace, 2 games behind the Stars, the nearest club above the drop.

Through 40 games they were 18-22, scoring 206 runs and allowing 215 (-9). As of the May 13 export they are 10th in the Western Conference, 8 games back, and 14th of 20 in the ABL. Runs scored and allowed like that typically buy about 19 wins in 40 games. They are 2-6 in one-run games and 1-3 in extra innings.

The hitters

Jim Thome has 49 hits, 32 walks and 42 strikeouts in 175 trips; in 2001 he had a 1.040 OPS over 644 PA. The best bat has belonged to Victor I. Diaz, 20, who is hitting .351/.403/.597 with 9 home runs and 25 RBI in 150 plate appearances. Jim Thome leads the club with 10 home runs. Tony Graffanino has 12 steals (caught 4 times). As a team they are hitting .292/.360/.458, 11th of 20 in the ABL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 49 home runs. The surprise is Tony Graffanino. His 2001, by the export's count, was a .777 OPS over 169 plate appearances; this year it is .931. Players do not usually get 154 points better by accident, though they do sometimes get there by April. The disappointment, at least so far, is Adam Dunn: a .948 OPS in 2001, .780 through 173 plate appearances this year.

The pitchers

In the rotation, Rick Reed has been the steadiest: 1-1 with a 2.91 ERA, 28 strikeouts and 6 walks in 34.0 innings. Alejandro Romero (3.82 ERA in 37.2 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.10 ERA, 10th of 20 in the ABL; starters 4.60, relievers 5.89. Kerry Ligtenberg has 10 saves without a blown save. In the rotation, Sean Douglass has a 6.69 ERA over 35.0 innings.

Close games are where baseball goes to flip coins, and the Golden Eagles have flipped a lot of them: 8 one-run games in their first 40, and they are 2-6 in them.

The best and worst of it

The best night was May 10, a 13-1 win over the Coquis. The worst was Apr 21, a 14-1 loss to the Royeaux. Their best 10-game stretch went 5-5 (Apr 1 to Apr 10); their worst, 2-8 (Apr 7 to Apr 17).

Some details worth knowing. Home crowds have averaged 20,138 over 23 dates in a park that holds 20,576, so the seats are mostly spoken for. When they score three runs or fewer they are 3-15; when they score six or more, 12-4. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

What next

The next stretch is 10 games (4 at home) against the Knights, the Loons, the Pirates, the Reds. Those clubs are 185-215 combined, a .463 winning percentage, so this is where a record can get fatter. One more thing to watch: Jim Thome is on the milestone watch, closing on 300 home runs.
