The Season So Far · ABL · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

Mexico City's Bullpen Is as Weak as Advertised, and the Golden Eagles Are in Last

The Golden Eagles came in with an extremely weak bullpen. Jorge Julio is 0-5, the team is 2-6 in one-run games, and Mexico City has the ABL's second-highest relegation odds.

Mexico City Golden Eagles · 18-22 · 206 runs scored, 215 allowed

Mexico City was built for power. Jim Thome and Adam Dunn would anchor a flawed, power-heavy lineup, and that lineup would have to carry an extremely weak bullpen in a tiny ballpark at altitude, a hitters' haven in more ways than one. The pitching got a D, the preseason consensus ranked the Golden Eagles 14th, and the projections had them at 75-76.

They're 18-22, last in the Western Conference, though only eight games out of first. The site gives them a 44.5% chance of relegation, second-highest in the ABL behind Austin.

The bullpen

The rotation hasn't been the problem. Its 4.60 ERA is respectable for this league. Joey Hamilton has a 4.12 ERA and threw 7.1 shutout innings against Baltimore on April 12. Rick Reed, one of the two starters who were all right in 2001, missed most of April hurt and has a 2.91 ERA in five starts since, with 28 strikeouts and six walks. Alejandro Romero, 24, is at 3.82.

The bullpen has been the problem, as feared. Its ERA is 5.89. Jorge Julio, one of the two headline relievers, is 0-5 with a 9.50 ERA and two blown saves. Kerry Ligtenberg has 10 saves and a 3.94 ERA. Mexico City is 2-6 in one-run games and 1-3 in extra innings. It has blown three-run leads three times and lost 10-9 in Kansas City on April 30 after leading by five. With a -9 run differential, the Golden Eagles should have about one more win than they do.

The power

Thome has been as advertised, hitting .345 with 10 homers, 32 walks and a 172 OPS+. Dunn has been slower. He's hitting .245 with a 93 OPS+, and though he seemed to snap out of it with two homers in an 8-5 win over Austin on April 18, and then drove in five in a 14-11 comeback over the Violets two days later, he's still below average. The young hitter carrying the lineup is a 20-year-old right fielder, Victor Diaz, who is hitting .351 with nine home runs. Tony Graffanino is hitting .353, and on April 5 he fell a double short of the cycle against Tampa Bay. Aaron Boone and Neifi Perez have OPS+ marks of 84 and 74.

For a power lineup in a hitters' park, the results are strange. Mexico City ranks 16th in runs per game and seventh in home runs, and it's 11-12 at home.

Lately

The Golden Eagles started 5-1, then lost four straight, including 11-3 and 14-2 losses in Salt Lake in which the teams hit seven home runs. They've lost all three games to Salt Lake, outscored 29-8, and all three to Montreal, outscored 23-2. After a five-game losing streak from May 3 to 8, they've won four of six, including two straight at home over Las Vegas. Next come three in New York, three at home against Minnesota and three in Pittsburgh.
