Roundup · 2002-04-07 · By Alexander Hadley · Alexander's league doc, daily roundups

Roundup, April 7: Oakland 5, Washington 3

3 tiers (MLB, ABL, FBL), 28 games noted.

MLB Daily highlights

  • Oakland 5, Washington 3: Tom Glavine solid; Washington homers by Travis Hafner and Damian Miller. Box: OAK 5, WCB 3
  • Miami 3, Arizona 2: Tony McKnight strong for six; bullpen locked it down yet again. Box: MIA 3, ARI 2
  • Seals over Mariners: Dave Burba delivered another sharp start; Tony Bautista had two RBI doubles. Box: SEA 4, SFS 9
  • Cubs 4, Astros 3: Mike Thurman went 7.2; Brandon Kolb and Eddie Guardado closed; Shawn Green hit a 2-run HR. Box: CHC 4, HOU 3
  • Braves 3, Yankees 2 (15): a marathon with almost no offense. Atlanta used five relievers; bullpen allowed hits but walked seven and still kept the damage minimal. It ended with a walk-off HR by Esteban Germán in the 15th. Box: NYY 2, ATL 3 (15)
  • Cardinals 8, Wolverines 0: Horacio Ramírez dominated; Raúl Ibañez went 5-for-5 with two HR. Box: DET 0, STL 8
  • Dodgers rally late vs Brooklyn: two runs off reliever Chris George after being shut down by starter Eric Hilgers. Box: LAD 3, BrGo 2
  • Blue Jays 6, Griffins 4: Steve Cox: two doubles and a HR; bullpen work from Scott Sauerbeck to finish. Box: TOR 6, DAL 4
  • Phillies 5, Brewers 2: big 4-run 6th; starter Runelvys Hernandez went 7.1 strong. Box: PHI 5, MIL 2

ABL Daily highlights

  • Nashville’s first win: down 4–1, scored three in the 9th to beat Mexico City 9–6. Huge HR from Bobby Abreu and Russell Branyan; the hero was Michael Tucker (four singles + clutch 2-run double in the 9th). Box: NASH 9, MEXC 6
  • Montreal 5, Minnesota 4: bullpen held; Rafael Palmeiro key bat. Box: MIN 4, MON 5
  • San Juan 8, New Orleans 3: Todd Walker set the tone (three singles, two walks). Box: SJC 8, NOLA 3
  • Anaheim 9, New York 2: Sterling Hitchcock dominated 6.2; 6 K, 0 BB. Box: NYK 2, LAA 9
  • Salt Lake 6, Cleveland 3: pitching—especially bullpen—held firm. Box: SLB 6, CLE 3
  • San Diego beat Cincinnati: Dustin Mohr drove in six with a grand slam + 2-run double. Box: SDP 9, CIN 5
  • Tampa walk-off sac fly: edged Southside after a late rally; David Bush had gone 7.1 earlier. Box: SBS 2, TBR 3
  • Pittsburgh 7, Austin 2: Tomokazu Ohka stayed hot (8 IP, 9 K). Box: AUS 2, PIT 7
  • Kansas City offensive burst: doubles everywhere; Vegas starter Brandon Duckworth couldn’t survive. Box: LVG 2, KCM 9
  • Orioles 1, Portland 0 (10): Rey Ordoñez RBI double in the 10th. Guillermo Mota wobbled but escaped with help—Ordoñez threw out the tying run at the plate. Box: BAL 1, PoBe 0 (10)

FBL Daily highlights

  • Alaska 5, Honolulu 4: dramatic 2-run HR in the 8th; shaky 9th, but survived. Box: ALSK 5, HONO 4
  • Hoboken crushed Eugene: 17 hits and three HR. Box: EUGL 4, HOBK 8
  • Richmond 10, Hartford 9: a thriller with lead changes and a walk-off homer off Tim Spooneybarger; Reggie Taylor (3-for-4, HR, 3 RBI) and Bret Boone (two HR) did the damage. Box: HYG 9, RiFS 10
  • Santo Domingo 10, Orlando 6: even a big Alfonso Soriano game (3-run HR + three hits) couldn’t save Orlando. Box: ORLJ 6, SDG 10
  • Savannah 4, San Antonio 1: Hideo Nomo went 6.1, 1 ER. Box: SARe 1, SaSa 4
  • Carolina 3, Albuquerque 1: Terrence Long HR; Frank Castillo went 8.1, 1 ER. Box: ALBQ 1, CDT 3
  • Monterrey stayed undefeated: Chris Capuano went eight strong. Box: SACR 2, MONT 4
  • Inland Empire 6, Louisville 4: 5-run 6th capped by Timo Pérez 3-run HR. Box: LoBa 4, SB66 6
  • Oklahoma City 8, Vancouver 7: Ben Grieve 3-run double provided the margin; Hollandsworth doubled again. Box: OKC 8, VANC 7
