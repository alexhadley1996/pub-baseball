40-Game Check-In · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

The Flying Squirrels Have a Colbrunn Problem (the Good Kind)

Richmond Flying Squirrels, 19-21 through 40 games

What does a team do with a hitter who is reaching base at a .430 clip and slugging .777? If it is the Flying Squirrels, it goes 19-21 and hopes he does not notice.

40 games, by the numbers

  • 19-21 record
  • -16 run differential (192 scored, 208 allowed)
  • 8-4 in one-run games
  • 9-8 at home
  • 10-13 on the road
  • 5.15 / 4.14 starter / reliever ERA
  • 45 home runs
  • 44 stolen bases

Through 40 games they were 19-21, scoring 192 runs and allowing 208 (-16). As of the May 13 export they are 7th in the East Group, 5½ games back, and 12th of 20 in the FBL. Runs scored and allowed like that typically buy about 19 wins in 40 games. They are 8-4 in one-run games and 2-2 in extra innings.

Greg Colbrunn has 50 hits, 11 walks and 13 strikeouts in 142 trips. Doug Mientkiewicz has been the Flying Squirrels hitter opponents least want to see: .354/.417/.486, 2 homers, 20 driven in. Greg Colbrunn leads the club with 13 home runs. Eric Owens has 12 steals without being caught. As a team they are hitting .280/.335/.446, 10th of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 45 home runs.

Brian Anderson leads the starters with a 3.28 ERA over 60.1 innings (32 K, 11 BB). Geremi Gonzalez (4.15 ERA in 43.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 4.82 ERA, 15th of 20 in the FBL; starters 5.15, relievers 4.14. Antonio Osuna has 10 saves and 4 blown.

Then there is Todd Zeile, whose OPS has gone from .732 in 2001 to .879 now. Some of that is the sample. The question is how much. The disappointment, at least so far, is Bret Boone: a .950 OPS in 2001, .705 through 160 plate appearances this year. In the rotation, Steve Woodard has a 8.08 ERA over 39.0 innings, after a 5.20 ERA in 2001.

The Flying Squirrels season has a hole in it, seven games long, running from Apr 14 to Apr 20. Around the hole is a team that has gone 19-14 otherwise, which is the more useful number.

Odds and ends: Home crowds have averaged 19,322 over 17 dates in a park that holds 19,560, so the seats are mostly spoken for. When they score three runs or fewer they are 3-12; when they score six or more, 11-6. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

The best night was May 12, an 11-1 win over the Pharoahs. The worst was Apr 28, a 13-0 loss to the 66ers. Their best 10-game stretch went 9-1 (Apr 3 to Apr 13); their worst, 2-8 (Apr 14 to Apr 23).

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. They are 12th of 20 in the table, 2½ games behind the Larks for the last ladder place.

The next stretch is 10 games (4 at home) against the Spirit, the Larks, the Sultanes, the Makani. Those clubs are 216-172 combined, a .557 winning percentage, so the schedule is not doing them any favors. One more thing to watch: Greg Colbrunn is on the milestone watch, closing on 100 home runs. A quarter of a season tells you what a team is capable of. The other three quarters tell you what it is. For the Flying Squirrels, the interesting part starts now.
