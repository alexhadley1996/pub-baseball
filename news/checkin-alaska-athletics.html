40-Game Check-In · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

The Athletics Have to Earn Every Run

Alaska Athletics, 20-20 through 40 games

The Athletics have hit 27 home runs in 40 games, which is fewer than most of the clubs they play. They have to score some other way, every day, and they are 20-20 doing it.

Through 40 games they were 20-20, scoring 151 runs and allowing 155 (-4). As of the May 13 export they are 6th in the West Group, 6 games back, and 10th of 20 in the FBL. Runs scored and allowed like that typically buy about 20 wins in 40 games. They are 8-6 in one-run games and 1-0 in extra innings.

What is driving it

Their most productive hitter is Rontrez Johnson (CF, 25), at .312/.379/.478 with 6 HR. Rontrez Johnson has 11 steals without being caught. As a team they are hitting .256/.326/.368, 18th of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 27 home runs.

Matt Morris leads the starters with a 1.83 ERA over 68.2 innings (52 K, 20 BB). Jeff Suppan (2.11 ERA in 55.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 3.77 ERA, 1st of 20 in the FBL; starters 3.24, relievers 5.23. Rod Beck has 10 saves and 2 blown.

Nobody should have expected Todd Pratt to be hitting .237/.346/.404. He posted a .628 OPS in 212 plate appearances in 2001. Forty games is enough to notice and not enough to be sure. The weakest regular bat has been Mike Benjamin, at .219/.233/.266 in 134 plate appearances.

Five shutouts in 40 games: a team that can do that on a regular basis has a floor most teams envy, and the Athletics are 20-20.

A couple of small, true things. Home crowds have averaged 13,514 over 18 dates in a park that holds 40,888, about 33% full. There is room to be convinced.

The stakes

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. They are 10th of 20 in the table, 1½ games behind the Larks for the last ladder place.

The best night was Apr 26, an 8-1 win over the Gallos. The worst was Apr 20, an 8-2 loss to the Yard Goats. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (May 1 to May 11); their worst, 3-7 (Apr 20 to Apr 30).

Looking ahead

The next stretch is 10 games (6 at home) against the Mounties, the Twisters, the Bats, the Pharoahs. Those clubs are 214-183 combined, a .539 winning percentage, so the schedule is not doing them any favors. There is no luck to give back and none to collect: 20 wins, about 20 expected. What changes the picture from here is health and Rontrez Johnson, not arithmetic.
