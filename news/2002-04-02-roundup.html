Roundup, April 2: Marlins 5, Braves 2

3 tiers (MLB, ABL, FBL), 14 games noted. Lead: Marlins 5, Braves 2.

MLB Marlins 5, Braves 2 Box: MIA 5, ATL 2

Miami improved to 2–1 behind an avalanche from the top of the order: Jason Giambi, Cristian Guzmán, and Carl Crawford each had three hits, and Miami’s five runs were essentially their work. Giambi homered in the 1st; Crawford sparked things with extra bases and speed.

Starter Tony McKnight battled through 7 solid innings. After Mark Wohlers imploded in the 8th, Dan Miceli entered and delivered a 4-out save, striking out three of the four hitters he faced. Miami won comfortably despite the bottom half of the lineup going 0-for-16 with zero walks.
