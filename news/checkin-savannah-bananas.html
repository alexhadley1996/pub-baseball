40-Game Check-In · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

Bananas at 40: The Run That Made the Record

Savannah Bananas, 22-18 through 40 games

For eight straight games, from Apr 18 to Apr 26, the Bananas did not lose. Every season has a stretch that it will be remembered for, and the Bananas may have already had theirs.

Through 40 games they were 22-18, scoring 224 runs and allowing 219 (+5). As of the May 13 export they are 3rd in the East Group, 2½ games back, and 6th of 20 in the FBL. That run differential would usually produce a record around 20-20. They are 4-5 in one-run games and 2-0 in extra innings.

What is driving it

The best bat has belonged to Garret Anderson, 29, who is hitting .327/.366/.538 with 8 home runs and 41 RBI in 172 plate appearances. Jeff DaVanon has 9 steals (caught 1 time). As a team they are hitting .289/.353/.467, 4th of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 50 home runs.

The rotation's anchor is Barry Zito: 9 starts, 54.2 innings, 3.79 ERA. Hideo Nomo (4.69 ERA in 48.0 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.16 ERA, 19th of 20 in the FBL; starters 5.24, relievers 5.02. Steve Kline has 9 saves without a blown save.

Nobody should have expected Damian Rolls to be hitting .296/.343/.432. He posted a .633 OPS in 249 plate appearances in 2001. Forty games is enough to notice and not enough to be sure. In the rotation, Robert Ramsay has a 9.91 ERA over 26.1 innings.

Geography has been destiny for the Bananas. In 19 home games they have won 14; in 21 road games, 8. The 22-18 record is an average of two very different moods.

A couple of small, true things. When they score three runs or fewer they are 0-10; when they score six or more, 16-5. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

The stakes

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. They are the 6th seed today: in the ladder, 3 games out of an automatic promotion spot and 1 game clear of the first team outside.

The best night was Apr 2, a 13-4 win over the Juicers. The worst was May 3, a 14-3 loss to the Twisters. Their best 10-game stretch went 9-1 (Apr 18 to Apr 28); their worst, 2-8 (Apr 2 to Apr 12).

Looking ahead

The next stretch is 10 games (6 at home) against the Larks, the Sultanes, the Makani, the Gallos. Those clubs are 213-178 combined, a .545 winning percentage, so the schedule is not doing them any favors. There is no luck to give back and none to collect: 22 wins, about 20 expected. What changes the picture from here is health and Garret Anderson, not arithmetic. None of this is settled, which is the point of May. But the Bananas have given us enough to argue about, and that is all you can ask of 40 games.
