Gallos at 40: Two Halves, One Record

Santo Domingo Gallos, 21-19 through 40 games

Split the Gallos season in half and you get two different arguments. The first half (13-7) says one thing; the second (8-12) says please disregard the first half.

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. They are 8th of 20 in the table, ½ games behind the Larks for the last ladder place.

Through 40 games they were 21-19, scoring 208 runs and allowing 207 (+1). As of the May 13 export they are 4th in the East Group, 3½ games back, and 8th of 20 in the FBL. That run differential would usually produce a record around 20-20. They are 5-2 in one-run games and 2-0 in extra innings.

The hitters

The best bat has belonged to Ryan Freel, 26, who is hitting .308/.400/.445 with 1 home runs and 14 RBI in 170 plate appearances. Ryan Freel has 15 steals (caught 1 time). As a team they are hitting .274/.348/.411, 14th of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 32 home runs. The surprise is Ricky Ledee. His 2001, by the export's count, was a .654 OPS over 272 plate appearances; this year it is .789. Players do not usually get 135 points better by accident, though they do sometimes get there by April.

The pitchers

Javier Vazquez leads the starters with a 3.93 ERA over 66.1 innings (64 K, 12 BB). Jason Jennings (4.04 ERA in 42.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.05 ERA, 16th of 20 in the FBL; starters 5.45, relievers 4.29. Kyle Farnsworth has 6 saves without a blown save. In the rotation, Cal Eldred has a 8.49 ERA over 41.1 innings.

The best and worst of it

The best night was Apr 13, a 14-5 win over the Yard Goats. The worst was May 4, an 11-1 loss to the Larks. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (Apr 1 to Apr 10); their worst, 4-6 (Apr 15 to Apr 25).

Odds and ends: Home crowds have averaged 27,217 over 19 dates in a park that holds 68,500, about 40% full. There is room to be convinced. When they score three runs or fewer they are 1-12; when they score six or more, 13-3. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

What next

The next stretch is 10 games (6 at home) against the Disco Turkeys, the Makani, the Knights, the Bananas. Those clubs are 172-225 combined, a .433 winning percentage, so this is where a record can get fatter. There is no luck to give back and none to collect: 21 wins, about 20 expected. What changes the picture from here is health and Ryan Freel, not arithmetic.
