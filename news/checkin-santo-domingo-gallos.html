The Season So Far · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

Santo Domingo Has Five Walk-Off Wins and a +1 Run Differential

Alexander expected the Gallos to win with Javier Vazquez's rotation and lose late with a weak bullpen. The rotation has a 5.45 ERA, the bullpen has been better, and close games have put Santo Domingo at 21-19.

Santo Domingo Gallos · 21-19 · 208 runs scored, 207 allowed

In March Alexander ranked Santo Domingo ninth in the FBL and predicted 73-78. He described the lineup as "brimming with young undeveloped talent" like Victor Martinez. On the mound, he wrote, "Javier Vazquez's start turn has him leading a solid FBL rotation, though they lack a real bullpen behind them." His personalities report listed the Gallos among its "motivation risks."

Motivation hasn't been a problem late in games. Santo Domingo is 21-19, fourth in the East Group and 3.5 games behind Louisville. The Gallos have scored 208 runs and allowed 207, which normally means a .500 team. They're a game better than that because they've won five walk-offs, gone 5-2 in one-run games and 2-0 in extra innings. The site gives them a 41.2% chance at the playoffs.

Close games

That pattern showed up early. They won their first four games, starting with an 11-inning walk-off on opening day. On April 2, according to that day's roundup, "five in the 8th; key blows from Ryan Freel and Orlando Hudson" beat Richmond. On April 6 against Orlando, "Javier Vázquez went 8.1 dominant" and the game ended on a sacrifice fly for a 2-1 walk-off. On April 14, Hartford's Ryan Vogelsong struck out 10 and left with a lead. The week-three notes described the result as "a four-run ninth from the Gallos," another walk-off.

Those notes also said "nearly every game has felt tight. They haven't shown much 'blowout gear' yet, and the lineup has been top-heavy," with "four regulars sitting below a 90 OPS+." Three are still there: Hudson (84), Brian Hunter (74) and third baseman Kevin Orie (68). Martinez, 23, has a .356 on-base percentage and a 93 OPS+. Freel has been the most dependable hitter, at .308/.400/.445 with 15 steals in 16 tries and 35 runs scored. Luis Polonia, 38, whom the notes called "excellent (OPS over 1.000)," has cooled a little to .361/.397/.583 in part-time play. On April 8 in Oklahoma City he had four hits and three RBI.

The rotation and the bullpen

Vazquez has held up his end. He has 64 strikeouts and 12 walks in 66.1 innings, a 3.93 ERA, and 10 strikeouts in each of two recent starts: a complete game in Albuquerque on April 28 and 8.1 scoreless innings against Hoboken on May 8. The rest of the rotation hasn't been solid. Cal Eldred, 34, is 0-3 with an 8.49 ERA and has allowed 10 home runs in 41.1 innings. Kevin Jarvis has a 6.03 ERA and Darren Dreifort 5.74, though Dreifort is 4-1. The rotation as a whole has a 5.45 ERA, and the team has allowed 52 home runs, more than any staff in the FBL.

The bullpen has been better than the "lack a real bullpen" warning suggested. Its ERA is 4.29, more than a run better than the rotation's. Kyle Farnsworth has six saves, a 3-0 record and a 1.54 ERA. Heath Bell has a 2.66 ERA. Craig Dingman (8.16) has been the exception.

Next

The Gallos are 12-7 at home and 9-12 on the road. They're coming off a series in Monterrey where they lost a five-run lead on Sunday and lost 9-8 on a walk-off, for once on the wrong end of one. Carolina visits next, followed by three games in Honolulu and a home series against last-place Charlotte.
