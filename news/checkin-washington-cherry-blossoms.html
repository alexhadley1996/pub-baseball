Cherry Blossoms at 40: Home Is Where the Wins Are

Washington Cherry Blossoms, 20-20 through 40 games

Ask the Cherry Blossoms where they would like to play tomorrow and the answer should be obvious. 13-5 at home. 7-15 on the road. Home cooking is real, or at least it has been for six weeks.

Through 40 games they were 20-20, scoring 184 runs and allowing 184 (+0). With 41 games in the books as of May 13 they are 20-21, 6th in the East Division, 5 games back, and 12th of 20 in MLB. Runs scored and allowed like that typically buy about 20 wins in 40 games. They are 4-2 in one-run games and 3-1 in extra innings.

What is driving it

Their most productive hitter is Travis Hafner (1B, 24), at .312/.394/.549 with 7 HR. Jose Hernandez leads the club with 8 home runs. As a team they are hitting .269/.333/.435, 5th of 20 in MLB by OPS through each club's first 40, with 43 home runs.

The rotation's anchor is Luke Hudson: 8 starts, 45.1 innings, 3.38 ERA. Johan Santana (4.04 ERA in 62.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 4.31 ERA, 16th of 20 in MLB; starters 4.51, relievers 3.79.

The surprise is Marlon Anderson. His 2001, by the export's count, was a .758 OPS over 574 plate appearances; this year it is .881. Players do not usually get 122 points better by accident, though they do sometimes get there by April. The weakest regular bat has been Endy Chavez, at .186/.230/.279 in 149 plate appearances.

A couple of small, true things. When they score three runs or fewer they are 2-16; when they score six or more, 14-0. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

The stakes

MLB has no wild card and no consolation prize: division winners play for the title, the last-place club in each division goes down to the ABL. The Cherry Blossoms are 5 games behind the Marlins in the East Division. The floor is 6 games below them, where the Braves currently stand on relegation pace.

The best night was May 1, an 11-2 win over the Blue Jays. The worst was May 12, a 9-1 loss to the Rockies. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (Apr 22 to May 2); their worst, 3-7 (Apr 2 to Apr 11).

Looking ahead

The next stretch is 10 games (5 at home) against the Astros, the Brewers, the Griffins, the Gothams. Those clubs are 201-209 combined, a .490 winning percentage, so it will mostly be up to them. There is no luck to give back and none to collect: 20 wins, about 20 expected. What changes the picture from here is health and Travis Hafner, not arithmetic. None of this is settled, which is the point of May. But the Cherry Blossoms have given us enough to argue about, and that is all you can ask of 40 games.
