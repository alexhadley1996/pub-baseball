The Solons Have a Huff Problem (the Good Kind)

Sacramento Solons, 25-15 through 40 games

Somewhere in the Solons lineup is a 1B named Aubrey Huff who seems to have decided that pitchers are optional: .410/.450/.660, 8 home runs. The rest of the club is 25-15 around him.

Aubrey Huff has 64 hits, 12 walks and 17 strikeouts in 169 trips; in 2001 he had a .660 OPS over 434 PA. The best bat has belonged to Olmedo Saenz, 31, who is hitting .293/.337/.516 with 8 home runs and 28 RBI in 169 plate appearances. David Dellucci leads the club with 8 home runs. On the bases, Kenny Lofton is 8-for-10 stealing. As a team they are hitting .293/.350/.460, 7th of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 45 home runs.

Brandon Knight leads the starters with a 1.96 ERA over 55.0 innings (36 K, 27 BB). Terry Mulholland (2.98 ERA in 48.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 3.94 ERA, 3rd of 20 in the FBL; starters 3.46, relievers 4.94. Closing has mostly meant Octavio Dotel: 13 saves, 5.89 ERA.

Through 40 games they were 25-15, scoring 216 runs and allowing 168 (+48). As of the May 13 export they are 2nd in the West Group, 1 game back, and 2nd of 20 in the FBL. By the old Pythagorean arithmetic, that is a 25-win team so far. They are 4-3 in one-run games and 1-3 in extra innings.

That 1.110 OPS is also the surprise. In 2001, per the export, Aubrey Huff managed .660 over 434 plate appearances, which is a long way from here.

A couple of small, true things. When they score three runs or fewer they are 5-8; when they score six or more, 13-4. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks. Kenny Lofton has walked 24 times and struck out 21, a ratio that belongs to a different, more patient century.

The best night was May 13, a 14-1 win over the Knights. The worst was Apr 4, a 12-7 loss to the Larks. Their best 10-game stretch went 8-2 (Apr 19 to Apr 29); their worst, 4-6 (May 1 to May 11).

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. Right now the Solons hold the 2nd seed, an automatic promotion place, ½ games clear of the third seed.

What comes next: 10 games starting May 14, 6 of them at home, against the 66ers, the Mounties, the Twisters, the Bats, opponents with a combined 195-204 record (.489). Neither a gift nor an ambush. The record matches the runs, so there is no regression bill coming and no hidden credit either. The next 122 games will mostly be about whether Aubrey Huff keeps doing this.
