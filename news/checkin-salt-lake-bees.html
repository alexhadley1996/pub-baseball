40-Game Check-In · ABL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

One Run at a Time in Salt Lake

Salt Lake Bees, 19-21 through 40 games

The Bees have played twelve games decided by one run. They are 9-3 in them. Nobody is quite sure how much of that is skill, which is what makes it the most interesting thing about a 19-21 team.

Through 40 games they were 19-21, scoring 223 runs and allowing 245 (-22). As of the May 13 export they are 8th in the Western Conference, 7 games back, and 13th of 20 in the ABL. By the old Pythagorean arithmetic, that is a 18-win team so far. They are 9-3 in one-run games and 2-1 in extra innings.

What is driving it

Brad Fullmer has been the Bees hitter opponents least want to see: .379/.458/.683, 11 homers, 37 driven in. As a team they are hitting .304/.372/.469, 4th of 20 in the ABL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 40 home runs.

The rotation's anchor is Pat Strange: 8 starts, 37.1 innings, 6.75 ERA. John Patterson (6.97 ERA in 50.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.97 ERA, 20th of 20 in the ABL; starters 7.25, relievers 4.32. Closing has mostly meant Ramiro Mendoza: 8 saves, 1.80 ERA.

And it is not the line anyone would have written for him: his 2001 OPS was .771 in 573 plate appearances. The weakest regular bat has been John Valentin, at .211/.299/.297 in 144 plate appearances. In the rotation, Dan Wright has a 7.68 ERA over 38.2 innings, after a 5.70 ERA in 2001.

Some details worth knowing. When they score three runs or fewer they are 2-11; when they score six or more, 14-4. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks. Brad Fullmer has walked 17 times and struck out 16, a ratio that belongs to a different, more patient century.

The stakes

The ABL is the middle rung, so it is the only league here where you can fall and climb in the same breath: each conference winner gets a bye, second and third play in, both finalists of the American Championship are promoted to MLB, and the bottom three are relegated to the FBL. They sit 8th in the Western Conference, 7 games back of the Black Sox. The drop is 1 game below them, where the Violets is the best of the clubs on relegation pace.

The best night was Apr 9, a 14-2 win over the Golden Eagles. The worst was Apr 13, a 13-3 loss to the Stars. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (Apr 7 to Apr 17); their worst, 3-7 (Apr 21 to May 1).

Looking ahead

What comes next: 10 games starting May 14, 7 of them at home, against the Coquis, the Gamblers, the Knights, the Loons, opponents with a combined 186-214 record (.465). That is a chance to bank wins. One more thing to watch: Brad Fullmer is on the milestone watch, closing on 100 home runs. Forty games is when baseball stops being a rumor and starts being a record. The Bees have one now. It is theirs to keep or to fix.
