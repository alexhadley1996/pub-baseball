Roundup · 2002-04-01 · By Alexander Hadley · Alexander's league doc, daily roundups

Roundup, April 1: Cherry Blossoms 5, Marlins 0

3 tiers (MLB, ABL, FBL), 22 games noted. Lead: Cherry Blossoms 5, Marlins 0.

MLB Cherry Blossoms 5, Marlins 0 Box: WCB 5, MIA 0

Washington bounced back immediately, blanking Miami 5–0 behind Andy Ashby, who authored a 2-hit shutout with 7 strikeouts.

MLB Daily roundup

  • Cubs 7, Phillies 6: Chicago stormed back late after falling behind 5–1. Todd Helton hit a 1-out, 3-run HR off Chris Hammond in the top of the 9th to seize the lead, and Eddie Guardado closed it. Box: CHC 7, PHI 6
  • Braves 3, Wolverines 1: Atlanta stayed hot behind a tight pitching duel led by Eric DuBose. Box: ATL 3, DET 1
  • Red Sox over Yankees: Boston rode a big game from Rich Aurilia (3-for-4 with a HR). Barry Bonds went 0-for-4 with two strikeouts. Box: NYY 2, BOS 7
  • Blue Jays jumped Rockies starter Brian Moehler early; Dmitri Young and Preston Wilson both hit two-run homers in a fast start. Box: COL 4, TOR 6
  • Cardinals 8, Brewers 4: St. Louis kept rolling. Through two games, leadoff man Shane Halter was hitting .667, with every hit a single. Box: MIL 4, STL 8
  • Rookie watch: Zack Greinke threw 7.3 innings, allowing three solo homers (including one to Manny Ramírez).
  • Seals: Rookie Jake Peavy delivered 6 IP, 2 ER, and Julio Mateo was already a bullpen machine—3 scoreless innings, 4 K, and he’d appeared in both games. Box: SFS 8, BrGo 2

ABL Opening Day notes

A tight, weird opening day: more than half the games decided by one run.

  • New Orleans 1, Tampa Bay 0: Kerry Wood tossed 8 scoreless (only 3 K) in a minimalist win. Box: NOLA 1, TBR 0
  • Baltimore held off Pittsburgh: Roy Oswalt went 7.1 and Guillermo Mota closed. Orioles drew 8 walks; Carlos Lee went 3-for-4, and John Vander Wal hit a 2-run HR. Box: BAL 7, PIT 6
  • Montreal beat Las Vegas in 13: Kevin Brown went 9 but didn’t get the win. Desi Relaford went 5-for-6; Andy Burkett went 0-for-6. Box: LVG 3, MON 4 (13)
  • Austin beat Charlotte in extras: Austin chipped away late and ultimately won a wild one. Box: NYK 6, AUS 7 (11)
  • Kansas City over Southside: comeback helped by a Larry Walker 3-run HR. Box: SBS 3, KCM 5
  • Portland over Nashville: Marlon Byrd set the table for Ángel Peña’s 2-run single in the 8th. Box: NASH 7, PoBe 9
  • Anaheim over San Juan: J.J. Hardy homered twice, including the tying HR in the 9th, in a dramatic win. Box: SJC 5, LAA 6

FBL Opening Day notes

Higher-scoring chaos.

  • Hoboken 9, Memphis 7: Roy Halladay got shelled but survived deep enough for Hoboken’s offense to outpace Memphis. Lou Merloni homered and drove in three in a losing effort. Box: MePh 7, HOBK 9
  • Savannah over Orlando: Barry Zito went 7.1, 1 ER. Box: ORLJ 3, SaSa 6
  • Alaska over Eugene: Matt Morris threw a CG shutout. Box: ALSK 7, EUGL 0
  • Hartford 5, Honolulu 2: Chuck Finley dominated into the 9th; Ellis Burks and Ronnie Belliard homered. Box: HYG 5, HONO 2
  • Monterrey debut: Monterrey exploded late, with a 6-run 7th capped by a Jeff Bagwell 2-run HR in a 9–5 win. Box: SARe 5, MONT 9
  • Vancouver crushed Inland Empire: Jamie Moyer threw 8 shutout; Todd Hollandsworth piled up doubles; Mark Bellhorn doubled and homered. Box: VANC 10, SB66 1
  • Carolina survived with thunder: included a Ken Griffey Jr. grand slam; Brendan Donnelly shut the door after late trouble. Box: LoBa 5, CDT 6
