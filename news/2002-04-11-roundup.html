Roundup · 2002-04-11 · By Alex Miller

Roundup, April 11: Seals 8, Yankees 6

1 tier (MLB), 8 games noted.

MLB Daily highlights

  • Seals 8, Yankees 6: another heavyweight classic. San Francisco’s lineup (including Andrés Galarraga and others) did damage early; Barry Bonds hit two HR and walked, but the Seals bullpen bent without breaking. Box: SFS 8, NYY 6
  • Dodgers won a 3–2 pitching duel: Mickey Callaway 7.1 shutout; Detroit’s Denny Neagle also threw 6.1 shutout but took the loss. Box: DET 2, LAD 3 (12)
  • Cardinals 10, Marlins 6: six early runs; Pat Borders (38) went 3-for-5 with a HR and a double, 4 RBI; Zack Greinke (18) gave six solid innings. Box: STL 10, MIA 6
  • Red Sox 3, Cherry Blossoms 0: Washington’s Andy Ashby pitched well again but got no help; Boston’s staff held them down. Box: WCB 0, BOS 3
  • Brewers 7, Cubs 6 (13): Julio Franco delivered the key hit in extras. Box: MIL 7, CHC 6 (13)
  • Astros 12, Rockies 7: Houston erased a 6–4 deficit with eight late runs; Alex Rodriguez had three RBI; Sammy Sosa hit a 2-run HR and reached base four times. Box: HOU 12, COL 7
  • D-backs 7, Mariners 3: Erubiel Durazo 3-run HR in the 8th. Box: ARI 7, SEA 3
  • Braves 6, A’s 0: starter Eric DuBose five shutout; bullpen finished it. Box: ATL 6, OAK 0
