Two Teams Called the Disco Turkeys

Carolina Disco Turkeys, 19-20 through 39 games

Here is the strangest thing about the Disco Turkeys at the 40-game mark: where they play seems to matter more than who they play. 13-8 at home, 6-12 on the road.

39 games, by the numbers

  • 19-20 record (39 games played)
  • +6 run differential (201 scored, 195 allowed)
  • 3-6 in one-run games
  • 13-8 at home
  • 6-12 on the road
  • 4.70 / 4.49 starter / reliever ERA
  • 41 home runs
  • 34 stolen bases

Through 39 games, all they had played when the export was taken on May 13, they were 19-20, with 201 runs scored and 195 allowed (+6). As of the May 13 export they are 5th in the East Group, 5 games back, and 11th of 20 in the FBL. Runs scored and allowed like that typically buy about 20 wins in 39 games. They are 3-6 in one-run games and 2-1 in extra innings.

Their most productive hitter is Ken Griffey Jr. (RF, 32), at .292/.368/.599 with 11 HR. Tom Goodwin has 12 steals (caught 2 times). As a team they are hitting .276/.350/.430, 11th of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 41 home runs.

John Burkett leads the starters with a 2.79 ERA over 58.0 innings (27 K, 20 BB). Frank Castillo (3.66 ERA in 51.2 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 4.63 ERA, 9th of 20 in the FBL; starters 4.70, relievers 4.49. Brendan Donnelly has 10 saves without a blown save.

The surprise might be Chad Tracy, who arrived without a 2001 top-tier line worth the name (under 150 plate appearances) and is hitting .314/.388/.471. The weakest regular bat has been Orlando Cabrera, at .237/.325/.311 in 154 plate appearances. In the rotation, Blake Stein has a 6.87 ERA over 38.0 innings, after a 4.74 ERA in 2001.

Odds and ends: When they score three runs or fewer they are 1-10; when they score six or more, 12-3. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

The best night was May 13, a 12-0 win over the Zephyrs. The worst was Apr 8, an 8-0 loss to the Larks. Their best 10-game stretch went 6-4 (Apr 6 to Apr 17); their worst, 2-8 (Apr 16 to Apr 26).

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. They are 11th of 20 in the table, 2 games behind the Larks for the last ladder place.

The next stretch is 10 games (2 at home) against the Gallos, the Knights, the 66ers, the Mounties. Those clubs are 173-227 combined, a .432 winning percentage, so this is where a record can get fatter. One more thing to watch: Ken Griffey Jr. is on the milestone watch, closing on 200 stolen bases. Forty games is when baseball stops being a rumor and starts being a record. The Disco Turkeys have one now. It is theirs to keep or to fix.
