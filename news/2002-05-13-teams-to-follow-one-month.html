One Month In: The Six Clubs to Follow, and Whose Odds Moved Most

From Alexander's Writing Topics list: Hartford, Carolina, Baltimore, Portland, Miami and Philadelphia at the one-month mark, plus the biggest gaps between his preseason standings and the site's current odds.

Alexander's writing calendar for 2002 names six clubs to follow all season: Hartford, Carolina, Baltimore, Portland, Miami and Philadelphia. His one-month topics are "chosen teams from each league to follow" and "biggest changes in playoff probability." Here are both, through May 12. The playoff odds are the site's own projection; there are no preseason odds to compare against, so "change" here means the distance between where Alexander put each club in March and where the simulation puts it now.

The six

Hartford Yard Goats (FBL, 22-17). Predicted 87-64; on pace for something close. The offense leads the FBL in runs per game. The worry Alexander raised in March, that the young players would not carry enough weight, has partly come true: Adam LaRoche is hitting .246/.276/.391 (OPS+ 71) and is at -0.9 WAR. Gary Sheffield and Randy Winn have covered for it. Site odds: 64.1% playoffs, 24.8% promoted.

Carolina Disco Turkeys (FBL, 19-20). His write-up expected 88-63 and a pitching staff that would need help; his standings had 74-77. They have allowed 195 runs, fewer than they have scored (201), and John Burkett (6-1, 2.79) and Frank Castillo (7-0) have records that outrun their FIPs (4.40 and 4.44). Ken Griffey Jr. has 11 homers. Site odds: 20.4% playoffs, 4.2% promoted.

Baltimore Orioles (ABL, 17-23). Predicted 79-72, with Alexander's note that the pitching depth would be the problem. It has been the offense: Baltimore ranks 18th in runs per game and 20th in home runs and steals, while the staff is third in ERA. They have scored and allowed exactly 183 runs, so 17-23 is three games worse than their runs say. Roy Oswalt is 2-5. Site odds: 12.1% playoffs, 12.2% relegated.

Portland Beavers (ABL, 23-17). Covered in the ABL check-in: the preseason relegation favorite is in a playoff spot with a +29 run differential and the league's best bullpen ERA. Site odds: 46.4% playoffs, 3.6% relegated.

Miami Marlins (MLB, 25-16). Predicted 70-81, ninth in the East. They lead the division. They have outscored opponents 183-165, a Pythagorean record of 22-19, and have gone 3-7 in their last ten. Alexander's own 40-game piece credits their luck with runners in scoring position; the run differential agrees that some of it is luck. Site odds: 37.9% to win the East and reach the World Series.

Philadelphia Phillies (MLB, 20-21). Predicted 86-65 and first in the East. They have scored 199 and allowed 189, so they have been about a .500 team in every sense. They lead MLB in stolen bases; the rotation ranks 20th in ERA, which fits his March worry that the staff was "too inexperienced and unproven." Site odds: 3.7% to win the division, 1.9% relegated.

Biggest gaps between March and now

Ranked by how far each club's current projected win total sits from Alexander's predicted record (both over the full schedule):

  • Monterrey Sultanes (FBL): predicted 70 wins, projected 87. Best record in the FBL.
  • Portland Beavers (ABL): predicted 64, projected 81.
  • Miami Marlins (MLB): predicted 70, projected 83.
  • Detroit Wolverines (MLB): predicted 71, projected 82; 8-2 in their last ten.
  • Minnesota Loons (ABL): predicted 66, projected 77; first in the ABL in runs per game.
  • San Francisco Seals (MLB): predicted 75, projected 87, with the best run differential in MLB (+48).
  • Alaska Athletics (FBL): predicted 94, projected 75. The biggest drop on the board.
  • Richmond Flying Squirrels (FBL): predicted 88, projected 73.
  • Philadelphia Phillies (MLB): predicted 86, projected 75.
  • Washington Cherry Blossoms (MLB): predicted 86, projected 75.

Two of Alexander's MLB relegation picks are holding up: Atlanta (14-27) has a 57.5% chance of going down and the Dodgers (17-24) 37.8%. The third, St. Louis, is at 4.2%; Boston (16-25, 28.1%) has taken its place in the danger zone.

Projected win totals and odds are from the site's 10,000-run simulation on the Playoffs page. Predicted records are from Alexander's "Preseason Predictions - 2002" sheet.
