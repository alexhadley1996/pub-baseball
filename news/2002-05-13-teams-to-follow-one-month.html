One Month In: The Six Clubs to Follow, and Whose Odds Moved Most

Hartford, Carolina, Baltimore, Portland, Miami and Philadelphia at the one-month mark, plus the biggest gaps between the preseason projections and the site's current odds.

Six clubs came into the season as the ones to follow all year: Hartford, Carolina, Baltimore, Portland, Miami and Philadelphia. A month and a half in, two of them are in playoff position, one leads its division, and one has already blown up its own scouting report. Here is where each stands through May 12, followed by the clubs whose outlook has moved the most since March. The playoff odds come from the site's own simulation. There were no preseason odds to compare them with, so "moved" here means the distance between a club's preseason projection and where the simulation puts it now.

The six

Hartford Yard Goats (FBL, 22-17). The projections had Hartford at 87-64, and it's on pace for something close to that, with the best offense in the FBL by runs per game. The March worry, that the young players wouldn't carry enough of the load, has partly come true. Adam LaRoche has a 71 OPS+ and is at -0.9 WAR. Gary Sheffield and Randy Winn have covered for him. Site odds: 64.1% playoffs, 24.8% promotion.

Carolina Disco Turkeys (FBL, 19-20). The March preview expected 88-63 and a pitching staff that would need help. The projections said 74-77, and the record is splitting the difference. Carolina has scored 201 runs and allowed 195. John Burkett (6-1, 2.79 ERA) and Frank Castillo (7-0) have records that run well ahead of their FIPs, 4.40 and 4.44, and Ken Griffey Jr. has 11 homers. Site odds: 20.4% playoffs, 4.2% promotion.

Baltimore Orioles (ABL, 17-23). The projections had Baltimore at 79-72, with pitching depth the expected problem. Instead the problem has been the offense. The Orioles rank 18th in runs per game and last in home runs and steals, while the staff is third in ERA. They've scored and allowed exactly 183 runs, so 17-23 is three games worse than their runs say. Roy Oswalt is 2-5. Site odds: 12.1% playoffs, 12.2% relegation.

Portland Beavers (ABL, 23-17). The preseason relegation favorite is in a playoff spot, with a +29 run differential and the league's best bullpen ERA. Site odds: 46.4% playoffs, 3.6% relegation.

Miami Marlins (MLB, 25-16). Projected at 70-81, ninth in the East, Miami leads the division. It has outscored opponents 183-165, more like a 22-19 team, and it's 3-7 in its last 10. A lot of the early success came from timely hitting with runners in scoring position, and the run differential agrees that some of it was luck. Site odds: 37.9% to win the East and reach the World Series.

Philadelphia Phillies (MLB, 20-21). The projections had Philadelphia at 86-65 and first in the East. The March preview was warier, at 76-75, and it's been closer. The Phillies have scored 199 and allowed 189, about a .500 team in every sense. They lead MLB in stolen bases, and the rotation ranks 20th in ERA, which is exactly where the preview said the trouble would come from. Site odds: 3.7% to win the division, 1.9% relegation.

Biggest moves since March

Ranked by how far each club's current simulated win total sits from its preseason projection, both over the full schedule:

  • Monterrey Sultanes (FBL): projected for 70 wins, now 87, with the best record in the FBL.
  • Portland Beavers (ABL): projected for 64, now 81.
  • Miami Marlins (MLB): projected for 70, now 83.
  • Detroit Wolverines (MLB): projected for 71, now 82, after going 8-2 in their last 10.
  • Minnesota Loons (ABL): projected for 66, now 77, and first in the ABL in runs per game.
  • San Francisco Seals (MLB): projected for 75, now 87, with the best run differential in MLB (+48).
  • Alaska Athletics (FBL): projected for 94, now 75, the biggest drop on the board.
  • Richmond Flying Squirrels (FBL): projected for 88, now 73.
  • Philadelphia Phillies (MLB): projected for 86, now 75.
  • Washington Cherry Blossoms (MLB): projected for 86, now 75.

At the other end of the MLB table, the two clubs projected worst have played to form. Atlanta (14-27) has a 57.5% chance of relegation, and the Dodgers (17-24) are at 37.8%. The surprise entry is Boston, projected at 75-76 and now 16-25 with a 28.1% chance of going down.

Projected win totals and odds are from the site's 10,000-run simulation on the Playoffs page. Preseason figures are the OOTP preseason projections.
