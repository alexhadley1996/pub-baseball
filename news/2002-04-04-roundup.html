Roundup, April 4: Marlins 9, Braves 6

3 tiers (MLB, ABL, FBL), 23 games noted. Lead: Marlins 9, Braves 6.

MLB Marlins 9, Braves 6 Box: MIA 9, ATL 6

Miami moved to 4–1 in a game that didn’t truly ignite until later. Atlanta hit four home runs off Miami starter Rubén Quevedo, including Mo Vaughn’s 300th career homer, but Miami matched the power with late offense and clutch extra-base hits, including key damage from Lee Stevens. Dan Miceli earned another save, and Miami’s bullpen outdueled Atlanta’s late arms (José Valverde, Keith Foulke) when it mattered.
