Preseason Power Rankings: All 60 Clubs, Graded

Offensive and pitching scouting reports and letter grades for every MLB, ABL and FBL team, ranked 1 through 60.

Sixty clubs, two grades each, and a rank for every team inside its own league. The offensive and pitching grades are scouting judgments, not projections; the tier and rank are where I would bet. Montreal tops the ABL on a veteran lineup with no prospects and no apparent need for any, the Yankees lead MLB, and Alaska leads the FBL with a lineup that has, in my March words, "no holes."
