FBL · 2002-04-03 · By Frank DeLuca, New York Evening Star

Down Nine, Up One, and Sheffield in the Middle of It

Savannah led Hartford 9-0 after two innings behind Jose Vidro's grand slam. The Yard Goats scored four in the sixth and five in the seventh, Gary Sheffield homered and then singled in the go-ahead run, and Hartford won 11-10 to stay unbeaten.

HARTFORD, Conn. — There were 6,098 people at the ballpark downtown on Wednesday night, which is a small crowd, and most of them were still there at the end, which says something about Hartford.

What it says is that this town has decided, three games into the season, that its team is worth sticking around for. The Yard Goats trailed the Savannah Bananas 9-0 after two innings. They won 11-10.

“I’ve been in this game a long time,” Gary Sheffield said, “and you don’t see that very often. You see teams come back from four or five. Nine is different. Nine, the other team has to help you a little. They helped. We took it.”

Nine runs

Joe Mays started for Hartford and got five outs. Jeff DaVanon led off the game with a homer. Jose Vidro homered in the first and then, in the second, hit a grand slam that made it 6-0. By the end of the inning Savannah had scored seven, and Mays had given up nine runs in 1⅔ innings.

Vidro finished 2-for-5 with two homers and five RBI. DaVanon homered twice. In the Bananas’ dugout, by all accounts, there was dancing.

“We were having fun,” DaVanon said. “We have fun when we’re losing. You can imagine.”

The comeback

Nelson Figueroa came out of the Hartford bullpen and threw 4⅓ innings, allowing one run. It wasn’t spectacular. It was enough.

Sheffield, three games into his experiment at shortstop, hit a two-run homer in the third. The Yard Goats got four more in the sixth, the last two on Randy Winn’s homer. And in the seventh, against a Savannah bullpen that ran out of answers, they scored five: Ronnie Belliard doubled in a run, Chris Widger doubled in two, Ray Durham doubled in the tying run, and with two outs Sheffield singled home the go-ahead run off Gabe White.

Curt Leskanic pitched the ninth for the save.

The shortstop

The headlines in Hartford all spring have been about Sheffield’s contract, the clause that makes him a free agent the moment the Yard Goats move him off shortstop. On Wednesday the more interesting story was how comfortable he looked there. He made the plays. He made a diving stop in the eighth, according to the people who were watching closely, that prevented a run.

“I keep telling people,” he said. “I’m a shortstop. I’ve always been a shortstop. They just wouldn’t let me.”

Bananas manager Jon Gomez was asked whether he’d ever lost a nine-run lead.

“Not since last summer,” he said. “It’s not my favorite thing.”

Hartford is 3-0. Savannah, 2-1, plays the Yard Goats again on Thursday.
