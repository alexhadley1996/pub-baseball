Roundup, April 12: Marlins 9, Cardinals 4

3 tiers (MLB, ABL, FBL), 26 games noted.

MLB Daily highlights

  • Marlins 9, Cardinals 4: Carl Crawford had a 2-hit, 1-double game; Miami produced up and down the lineup. Box: STL 4, MIA 9
  • Cubs 5, Brewers 4: Melvin Mora three-hit game; Milwaukee threatened in the 9th before a double play ended it. Box: MIL 4, CHC 5
  • Phillies 7, Dallas 1: starter Runelvys Hernandez went 8.1 excellent. Box: DAL 1, PHI 7
  • Seals 10–3: beat the Yankees again in a pitchers’ duel; Dave Burba threw 6 shutout; Chris Benson was strong (7.2, 1 ER), but SF still won. Box: SFS 1, NYY 0
  • Brooklyn 3, Toronto 2: won on an Alex Cora clutch double in the 9th; starter Eric Hilgers and bullpen were heroic. Box: BrGo 3, TOR 2
  • A’s 8, Braves 3: Tom Glavine complete game; Gene Schall 3-run HR. Box: ATL 3, OAK 8
  • Mariners 9, D-backs 8: Seattle held on despite Arizona scoring six late; Robin Ventura had a single and a 3-run HR. Box: ARI 8, SEA 9
  • Wolverines 3, Dodgers 2: David Krohn led Detroit to the win. Box: DET 3, LAD 2
  • Astros 10, Rockies 2: three HR and a quick early lead. Box: HOU 10, COL 2

ABL Daily highlights

  • New Orleans 12, Knights 11 (10): seven-run 7th; Knights answered late; New Orleans tied it on a 2-run single by Eric Young, then walked it off in the 10th. Brian Roberts went 3-for-4 with 4 RBI. Box: NYK 11, NOLA 12 (10)
  • Cleveland beat San Diego 9–7: won it on a Pedro Feliz 3-run HR in the 9th. Box: SDP 7, CLE 9
  • Tampa 5, San Juan 2: starter Ben Sheets went eight strong (2 ER, 9 K); Tampa controlled it. Box: SJC 2, TBR 5
  • Nashville 4, Salt Lake 1: Matt Clement struck out 10. Box: SLB 1, NASH 4
  • Royeaux 8, Reds 2: Rafael Palmeiro hit two HR; Vicente Padilla was ejected after a big homer sequence. Box: MON 8, CIN 2
  • Southside 8, Las Vegas 5: late scoring. Box: LVG 5, SBS 8
  • Mexico City shut out Baltimore: Joey Hamilton threw 7.1 shutout. Box: BAL 0, MEXC 3
  • KC 8, Minnesota 5: HR from Jeff Bloom and Rey Langford; Fernando Viña chipped in. Box: MIN 5, KCM 8

FBL Daily highlights

  • Louisville 8, Oklahoma City 7: big day from Paul Lo Duca; Louisville’s Andruw Jones hit a 2-run HR off CC Sabathia. Box: LoBa 8, OKC 7
  • Hartford 9, Santo Domingo 4: early outburst; Greg Myers went 4-for-5, HR, 4 RBI; José Guillén added an RBI triple. Box: HYG 9, SDG 4
  • Richmond 5, Alaska 3: Todd Zeile went 3-for-3; Casey Blake went 4-for-4 with a 2-run HR in the loss. Box: ALSK 3, RiFS 5
  • Eugene 7, Charlotte 3 (10): Curt Schilling went 8.2 with 10 K and still didn’t get the win; Eugene broke it open with four in the 10th off Justin Speier, and Chuck Knoblauch went 4-for-5 with a homer. Box: EUGL 7, ChKn 3 (10)
  • Orlando rally win: came back to beat San Antonio 4–3. Box: SARe 3, ORLJ 4 (8)
  • Monterrey 9, Carolina 2 (April 13, game 1): Craig Biggio, Jeff Bagwell, Alex Ochoa combined 7-for-12, 7 R, 6 RBI. Box: MONT 9, CDT 2
  • Honolulu 6, Hoboken 3: three HR, including one by a red-hot Mike Kincaid; bullpen finished it. Box: HOBK 3, HONO 6
  • Inland Empire 6, Albuquerque 4: Carl Everett 3-for-4; Jeremy Giambi had a 2-run HR and a double off Al Leiter, who lasted 4.1. Box: ALBQ 4, SB66 6
  • Vancouver: Jamie Moyer stayed perfect, now 3–0, with only two runs allowed in 24 innings. Box: MePh 2, VANC 8
