The Season So Far · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

San Antonio Is 1-8 When Mark Buehrle Starts

Alexander ranked the Rebeldes third in the FBL and picked them for promotion. Josh Beckett and Juan Rincon have been excellent, but the rest of the club hasn't, and San Antonio is 17-23 with the worst run differential in the West Group.

San Antonio Rebeldes · 17-23 · 160 runs scored, 201 allowed

Alexander had high hopes for San Antonio in March. He ranked the Rebeldes third in the FBL, named them one of his three promotion picks, and graded the pitching B+: "Josh Beckett and Mark Buehrle make this a solid rotation for the FBL and young closer Juan Rincon leads a mediocre but not disastrous bullpen." His concern was the lineup: "the middle infield is a point of real problem but they have some exciting prospects in Justin Morneau and Jayson Werth."

The Rebeldes are 17-23, ninth in the West Group and nine games behind Monterrey. Their -41 run differential is the worst in the FBL. The site gives them a 3.7% chance at the playoffs.

Beckett and Buehrle

Beckett, 21, has lived up to the rotation's billing. He's 5-1 with a 2.24 ERA, 55 strikeouts in 56.1 innings, two home runs allowed and 2.0 WAR, tops on the team. On April 6 in Savannah, according to that day's roundup, he "outdueled Barry Zito." The week-three notes highlighted his eight shutout innings in Santo Domingo, a 2-0 win on April 20. On Friday he struck out 12 in 7.1 scoreless innings against Inland Empire.

Buehrle hasn't. He's 1-6 with a 5.80 ERA and has allowed seven home runs. San Antonio is 1-8 in his starts, and its only win came on April 22 in Alaska. In his first start, on April 2, Monterrey beat the Rebeldes 16-1. The team record is 5-4 when Beckett starts and 11-11 when anyone else besides Buehrle does. Carlos Hernandez, 22, has a 3.61 ERA. Jeff Fassero, 39, has a 6.05 ERA but a 3.76 FIP.

Rincon has been as good as advertised. He has 12 saves, no blown saves, 26 strikeouts and five walks in 17.2 innings, and hasn't allowed a home run. His FIP is 1.20. The bullpen around him has a 4.95 ERA, and San Antonio is 2-4 in extra innings.

The lineup

As predicted, the problem in the middle infield is second base, not shortstop. Julio Lugo has a 124 OPS+ at short. Second baseman Wilton Guerrero is hitting .240/.283/.295 with no home runs, a 51 OPS+ and -0.8 WAR. Third baseman Pete Zoccolillo has a 73. Morneau, 20, has 11 home runs and a 116 OPS+. The April 4 roundup noted "huge games from Justin Morneau" in a 10-9 win over Honolulu, when he went 3-for-3 with two homers. Werth, 22, has a .284 on-base percentage and a 68 OPS+. Brian Jordan, 35, has been the most valuable hitter at 1.9 WAR.

As a team, San Antonio has 276 strikeouts and 98 walks and ranks 18th in the FBL in runs per game. Some of the close games have still gone its way: the Rebeldes are 7-5 in one-run games, including an 8-7 comeback win in 11 innings over Hartford on April 16 and an 8-7 comeback in Charlotte on May 4.

Next

The Rebeldes lost two of three in Vancouver over the weekend, with Buehrle starting Monday's 7-3 loss. Oklahoma City visits next. Then San Antonio goes on the road for three games in first-place Louisville and three in Memphis. To reach Alexander's 73-78 prediction, they'd have to win 56 of their last 111 games. Beckett will probably keep his end of it.
