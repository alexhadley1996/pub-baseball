The Season So Far · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

San Antonio Is 1-8 When Mark Buehrle Starts

The Rebeldes were ranked third in the FBL in the preseason consensus. Josh Beckett and Juan Rincon have been excellent, the rest of the club hasn't, and San Antonio is 17-23 with the worst run differential in the FBL.

San Antonio Rebeldes · 17-23 · 160 runs scored, 201 allowed

San Antonio came into the season with high hopes. The preseason consensus ranked the Rebeldes third in the FBL, and the pitching got a B+: Josh Beckett and Mark Buehrle made a solid rotation, and young closer Juan Rincon led a mediocre but not disastrous bullpen. The worry was the lineup, with real problems in the middle infield and two exciting prospects in Justin Morneau and Jayson Werth.

The Rebeldes are 17-23, ninth in the West Group and nine games behind Monterrey. Their -41 run differential is the worst in the FBL, and the site gives them a 3.7% chance at the playoffs.

Beckett and Buehrle

Beckett, 21, has been everything the rotation needed. He's 5-1 with a 2.24 ERA, 55 strikeouts in 56.1 innings, two home runs allowed and a team-high 2.0 WAR. On April 6 in Savannah he outdueled Barry Zito. On April 20 he threw eight shutout innings in Santo Domingo for a 2-0 win. On Friday he struck out 12 in 7.1 scoreless innings against Inland Empire.

Buehrle hasn't been. He's 1-6 with a 5.80 ERA and seven homers allowed, and San Antonio is 1-8 in his starts, the lone win on April 22 in Alaska. His first start set the tone: Monterrey beat the Rebeldes 16-1 on April 2. The team is 5-4 behind Beckett and 11-11 behind everyone other than Buehrle. Carlos Hernandez, 22, has a 3.61 ERA. Jeff Fassero, 39, has a 6.05 ERA but a 3.76 FIP.

Rincon has been as good as advertised: 12 saves, no blown saves, 26 strikeouts and five walks in 17.2 innings and no homers allowed, for a 1.20 FIP. The bullpen around him has a 4.95 ERA, and San Antonio is 2-4 in extra innings.

The lineup

The middle-infield problem turned out to be at second base. Julio Lugo has a 124 OPS+ at short. Wilton Guerrero, at second, is hitting .240 without a home run, with a 51 OPS+ and -0.8 WAR. Third baseman Pete Zoccolillo is at 73. Morneau, 20, has 11 homers and a 116 OPS+, and on April 4 he went 3-for-3 with two homers in a 10-9 win over Honolulu. Werth, 22, has a .284 on-base percentage. Brian Jordan, 35, has quietly been the most valuable hitter, at 1.9 WAR.

As a team San Antonio has 276 strikeouts and 98 walks and ranks 18th in the FBL in runs per game. Some close games have still gone its way. The Rebeldes are 7-5 in one-run games, including an 8-7 comeback win in 11 innings over Hartford on April 16 and an 8-7 comeback in Charlotte on May 4.

Next

The Rebeldes lost two of three in Vancouver over the weekend, with Buehrle starting Monday's 7-3 loss. Oklahoma City visits next, then San Antonio goes to first-place Louisville for three and Memphis for three. Just to reach its preseason projection of 73-78, it would have to win 56 of its last 111 games. Beckett will probably hold up his end.
