40-Game Check-In · ABL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

The Pirates Are the Median

Pittsburgh Pirates, 20-20 through 40 games

The Pirates have scored 232 runs and allowed 230. They are 20-20. If you wanted to build the most average baseball team possible, you could do worse than to copy this one, and you would probably have more fun doing it.

The ABL is the middle rung, so it is the only league here where you can fall and climb in the same breath: each conference winner gets a bye, second and third play in, both finalists of the American Championship are promoted to MLB, and the bottom three are relegated to the FBL. They sit 4th in the Eastern Conference, 3 games back of the Spiders. The drop is 2 games below them, where the Violets is the best of the clubs on relegation pace.

Through 40 games they were 20-20, scoring 232 runs and allowing 230 (+2). As of the May 13 export they are 4th in the Eastern Conference, 3 games back, and 9th of 20 in the ABL. That run differential would usually produce a record around 20-20. They are 4-7 in one-run games and 1-3 in extra innings.

The hitters

Their most productive hitter is Shannon Stewart (LF, 28), at .370/.466/.486 with 3 HR. Jay Gibbons leads the club with 10 home runs. On the bases, Jose Reyes is 22-for-25 stealing. As a team they are hitting .292/.349/.455, 14th of 20 in the ABL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 51 home runs. The surprise is Dan Wilson. His 2001, by the export's count, was a .708 OPS over 408 plate appearances; this year it is .862. Players do not usually get 154 points better by accident, though they do sometimes get there by April. On the other side of the ledger, Jeff Liefer is at .179/.231/.321, a long way from the .833 OPS he managed in 2001.

The pitchers

In the rotation, Steve Sparks has been the steadiest: 3-2 with a 4.94 ERA, 28 strikeouts and 21 walks in 51.0 innings. Bryan Rekar (5.47 ERA in 49.1 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.44 ERA, 13th of 20 in the ABL; starters 5.72, relievers 4.82. Closing has mostly meant B.J. Ryan: 9 saves, 3.14 ERA. In the rotation, Joel Pineiro has a 6.27 ERA over 56.0 innings, after a 2.03 ERA in 2001.

The best and worst of it

The best night was Apr 6, a 13-0 win over the Violets. The worst was Apr 13, a 22-4 loss to the Angels. Their best 10-game stretch went 8-2 (Apr 1 to Apr 10); their worst, 3-7 (Apr 17 to Apr 27).

Some details worth knowing. Shannon Stewart has walked 22 times and struck out 20, a ratio that belongs to a different, more patient century. They once scored 14 runs in a game (Apr 4, against the Padres); that one game is 6% of their season's runs.

What next

What comes next: 10 games starting May 14, 6 of them at home, against the Beavers, the Spiders, the Golden Eagles, the Stars, opponents with a combined 208-192 record (.520). Neither a gift nor an ambush. The record matches the runs, so there is no regression bill coming and no hidden credit either. The next 122 games will mostly be about whether Shannon Stewart keeps doing this.
