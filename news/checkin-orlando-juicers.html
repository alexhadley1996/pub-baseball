The Season So Far · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Site Staff

Ichiro Has Hit .420 Since Week Three. Orlando's Pitching Is Still Ugly.

The Juicers were ranked 19th, with a pretty horrible staff outside Byung-Hyun Kim, and Ichiro was still a preseason MVP candidate. After an 0-7 start, Orlando is 16-24.

Orlando Juicers · 16-24 · 174 runs scored, 206 allowed

The preseason consensus ranked Orlando 19th of 20 in the FBL, though the projections were kinder, at 77-74. The pitching review was one sentence long: a pretty horrible staff, except for Byung-Hyun Kim at closer. Ichiro Suzuki was one of Alex Miller's three preseason MVP candidates, and the Juicers were the only team with multiple true fan favorites, in a park where crowd energy matters.

The crowd hasn't had much to cheer. The Juicers lost their first seven games. By April 5 they were 0-5 with 11 runs scored, after a 1-0 loss to Richmond in a seven-inning game. The first win finally came on April 8 in Hartford, with a big game from Ryan Ludwick and Kim's first save. Orlando is 16-24 now, ninth in the East Group and 8.5 games back.

Ichiro

Ichiro had nudged his average up to .286 three weeks in. Since then he's 34-for-81, a .420 clip, and he's hitting .354 with 16 steals and a team-best 1.5 WAR. The MVP case has one problem: one home run and eight walks keep his OPS+ at 115. Matt Williams, 36, has been a legitimate middle-of-the-order force, with a 120 OPS+ and seven homers. Alfonso Soriano leads the team with eight, and on April 7 he had three hits and a three-run homer in a 10-6 loss in Santo Domingo. Sean Casey has a 95 OPS+.

The outfield has been as weak as it looked. Geronimo Berroa, 37, is hitting .188. Ludwick, in center, has no home runs and an 89 OPS+. Jared Sandberg has played some second base and hit .155 with 32 strikeouts in 91 plate appearances. Catcher Johnny Estrada has a 60 OPS+.

The staff

Orlando's 5.15 ERA ranks 18th of 20. Three weeks in, after the Juicers had allowed 28 runs in three games, the rotation looked thin beyond Brian Lawrence, and it still does. Lawrence has a 3.69 ERA and 43 strikeouts against 15 walks in 63.1 innings. He's 3-5, partly because of games like his April 17 complete game in Alaska, a 2-1 loss. Joe Kennedy, shelled as a rookie in Savannah on April 2, has settled at 4.85. Mark Hendrickson has a 6.00 ERA and has failed to finish the third inning twice. Clay Condrey has a 10.26 ERA in relief.

Kim, the one exception, has nine saves and no blown saves, but he's pitched only 9.1 innings. The bullpen ERA is 5.66, and it blew a five-run lead in Hoboken on April 23.

Lately

Orlando went 7-3 from April 8 to 18 and has gone 9-14 since. It finished the weekend by taking two of three from Louisville, the East leader, capped by Monday's 12-4 win behind Lawrence. The site gives the Juicers a 2.6% chance at the playoffs and projects about 68 wins. Next come three in Memphis, Albuquerque at home and three in Eugene.
