40-Game Check-In · FBL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

The Juicers, After the 7-Game Skid

Orlando Juicers, 16-24 through 40 games

Seven losses in a row is a long time. It is long enough for a team to try everything, and the Juicers had from Apr 1 to Apr 7 to do so. They are 16-24 now.

40 games, by the numbers

  • 16-24 record
  • -32 run differential (174 scored, 206 allowed)
  • 5-5 in one-run games
  • 9-9 at home
  • 7-15 on the road
  • 4.91 / 5.66 starter / reliever ERA
  • 39 home runs
  • 37 stolen bases

Through 40 games they were 16-24, scoring 174 runs and allowing 206 (-32). As of the May 13 export they are 9th in the East Group, 8½ games back, and 18th of 20 in the FBL. That run differential would usually produce a record around 17-23. They are 5-5 in one-run games.

Their most productive hitter is Matt Williams (3B, 36), at .305/.333/.532 with 7 HR. Alfonso Soriano leads the club with 8 home runs. On the bases, Ichiro Suzuki is 16-for-20 stealing. As a team they are hitting .270/.324/.424, 15th of 20 in the FBL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 39 home runs.

In the rotation, Brian Lawrence has been the steadiest: 3-5 with a 3.69 ERA, 43 strikeouts and 15 walks in 63.1 innings. Joe Kennedy (4.85 ERA in 42.2 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.15 ERA, 18th of 20 in the FBL; starters 4.91, relievers 5.66. Closing has mostly meant Byung-hyun Kim: 9 saves, 3.86 ERA.

In the rotation, Mark Hendrickson has a 6.00 ERA over 42.0 innings.

The fine print is fun here. When they score three runs or fewer they are 2-14; when they score six or more, 8-3. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

The best night was May 9, a 10-1 win over the Twisters. The worst was Apr 20, an 11-0 loss to the Solons. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (Apr 8 to Apr 18); their worst, 2-8 (Apr 1 to Apr 10).

The FBL is the bottom rung and nobody gets relegated from it; the whole point is the way up. The top seven make a ladder playoff, seeds one and two are promoted outright, and the winner of the Federal Promotion round makes three. They are 18th of 20 in the table, 5½ games behind the Larks for the last ladder place.

What comes next: 10 games starting May 14, 4 of them at home, against the Pharoahs, the Spirit, the Larks, the Sultanes, opponents with a combined 205-187 record (.523). Neither a gift nor an ambush. One more thing to watch: Matt Williams is on the milestone watch, closing on 1,000 runs. None of this is settled, which is the point of May. But the Juicers have given us enough to argue about, and that is all you can ask of 40 games.
