Roundup, April 9: D-backs 10, Marlins 6

3 tiers (MLB, ABL, FBL), 26 games noted.

MLB Daily highlights

A day of nail-biters: 7 of 10 games decided by one run.

  • D-backs 10, Marlins 6: Pat Hentgen strong for 6.2; Robin Ventura cleared the bases with a key double in the 8th; Matt Lawton hit a 3-run HR. Box: MIA 6, ARI 10
  • Seals 5, Mariners 4: Seattle 2B Marty Malloy had a huge day, but SF rallied late—Rondell White hit a 2-run HR, and Jimmy Rollins drew a walk-off walk. Box: SEA 4, SFS 5
  • Dodgers 4, Brooklyn 3: Dave Mlicki 6.1 quality. Box: LAD 4, BrGo 3
  • Yankees walk-off in 14 vs Braves: comeback fueled by a Bonds HR; finished by Carlos Baerga walk-off HR. Box: NYY 7, ATL 6 (14)
  • Cherry Blossoms wild comeback: scored five in the last three innings to win 7–6 after trailing 6–2. Box: OAK 6, WCB 7
  • Astros walk-off vs Cubs (12): won on a Tony Clark walk-off single. Box: CHC 5, HOU 6 (12)
  • Brewers 3, Phillies 1: Jae Spurgeon 8 strong; Carlos Beltrán three hits and a solo HR. Box: PHI 1, MIL 3
  • Red Sox 10, Rockies 9: Boston rallied late; Colorado bullpen (including Oliver Pérez) collapsed. Box: BOS 10, COL 9
  • Cardinals 3, Wolverines 0: Corey Lee threw eight shutout. Box: DET 0, STL 3

ABL Daily highlights

  • Austin 5, Cincinnati 4 (12): HR from Alex Díaz and Mike Piazza. Box: CIN 4, AUS 5 (12)
  • Las Vegas walk-off vs New Orleans: Desi Relaford walk-off single; bullpen threw 4.2 strong. Box: NOLA 5, LVG 6 (10)
  • Salt Lake 14, Mexico City 2: seven HR total; Val Pascucci and Gabe Gross drove in four each. Box: MEXC 2, SLB 14
  • Nashville 5, Tampa 4: Milton Bradley had a monster game (four hits, four RBI). Box: TBR 4, NASH 5
  • Orioles 7, Cleveland 4: Benny Agbayani hit his third HR; Brian Sweeney 7.2 strong. Box: CLE 4, BAL 7
  • Pirates 9, Royals 6: José Reyes (18) hit two HR and had four hits. Box: MON 6, PIT 9
  • KC 14, Knights 8: messy defense, bad pitching; 3 unearned runs. Box: KCM 14, NYK 8
  • Minnesota 10, Anaheim 3: Keith Ginter went 4-for-4 with 8 RBI, including a grand slam and multiple run-scoring doubles. Box: LAA 3, MIN 10
  • Padres 9, Portland 1: Phil Nevin drove in seven (3-run HR + grand slam). Box: PoBe 1, SDP 9

FBL Daily highlights

  • Carolina 5, Eugene 0: staff combined for a 4-hit shutout; Pablo Sánchez hit a 6th-inning grand slam. Box: CDT 5, EUGL 0
  • Monterrey 7, Hoboken 3: Craig Biggio went 4-for-5; Rob Quinlan 3-for-4 with a 2-run HR. Box: MONT 7, HOBK 3
  • Vancouver 12, Louisville 7: big day from 3B Deivi Cruz. Box: VANC 12, LoBa 7
  • Memphis walk-off vs Inland Empire: Josh Jenkins hit a 2-out, 3-run walk-off HR. Box: SB66 2, MePh 3
  • San Antonio 4, Richmond 2: starter Carlos Hernández 5.1 strong. Box: RiFS 2, SARe 4
  • Orlando 4, Hartford 3: 13-hit effort carried them. Box: ORLJ 4, HYG 3
  • Sacramento 1, Honolulu 0: scoreless until Olmedo Saenz hit a 9th-inning solo HR off Brad Lidge; it spoiled an incredible Jesse Foppert start. Box: HONO 0, SACR 1
  • Alaska 2, Savannah 1: bullpen brilliant; closer Rod Beck struck out the side in the 9th; Junior Spivey had four hits. Box: SaSa 1, ALSK 2
