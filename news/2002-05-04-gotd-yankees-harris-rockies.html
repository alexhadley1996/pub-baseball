MLB · 2002-05-04 · By Rob Lindqvist, Diamond Daily

The Other 37-Year-Old

Jose Canseco hit two home runs for Colorado, Barry Bonds hit one for the Yankees, and the game was decided by Lenny Harris, whose two-run homer in the eighth gave New York a 6-5 win.

NEW YORK — Saturday’s game at Yankee Stadium had three home runs from two famous men in their late 30s. Jose Canseco, 37, hit two of them for Colorado. Barry Bonds, 37, hit one for New York.

It was won by a fourth homer, from a third 37-year-old. With two outs in the bottom of the eighth and the Yankees down a run, Lenny Harris hit a two-run homer off Colorado’s Pat Neshek, and the Yankees beat the Rockies 6-5 in front of 36,633.

“Everybody’s waiting on Barry,” Harris said. “Sometimes the pitcher’s waiting on Barry too, and he forgets about the rest of us.”

Canseco’s day

Canseco gave Colorado a 1-0 lead with a two-out homer in the first off Yankees starter Brian Tollberg. In the fifth, Adam Hyzdu homered, and Canseco followed him with another to make it 5-2. They were Canseco’s ninth and 10th home runs.

Tollberg, who came in 4-1 with a 3.21 ERA, gave up 12 hits in seven innings, including all three homers. He also pitched through the seventh, retiring the side in order, which mattered later.

The Rockies’ other two runs came on Alex Gonzalez’s two-out, two-run single with the bases loaded in the third.

Bonds and the comeback

The Yankees got their first two runs early, on Bonds’ groundout in the first and Chan Perry’s single in the second. In the bottom of the fifth, with two outs and a runner on second, Bonds hit his 14th homer of the season off Colorado starter Chris Bootcheck to cut the lead to 5-4.

Bootcheck lasted six innings. Gus Gandarillas threw a scoreless seventh, and in the eighth Bill Pulsipher got Bonds on a fly ball before giving way to Neshek, a 21-year-old.

Neshek struck out Torii Hunter. Carlos Baerga singled. And Harris, who was hitting .319 coming in, hit the homer that won it.

“That’s what he does,” Yankees manager Joe Torre said. “He’s been doing it for 15 years. Lenny’s always ready.”

Harris finished 2-for-4 with a triple and the homer.

Bradford and Politte

Chad Bradford, the submariner, pitched the eighth for the Yankees and got the win. Cliff Politte pitched the ninth for the save. He walked Corey Hart to start it, then got two ground balls, the first of them a double play.

The Yankees are 17-16 and the Rockies 15-18. Canseco was 2-for-4 with two homers. Bonds was 1-for-4.

“Two homers and we lost,” Canseco said. “Write that down. That doesn’t happen to me very often.”
