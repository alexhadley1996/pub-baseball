40-Game Check-In · ABL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

What Martinez Is Doing in Cleveland

Cleveland Spiders, 23-17 through 40 games

Somewhere in the Spiders lineup is a 1B named Tino Martinez who seems to have decided that pitchers are optional: .416/.456/.714, 12 home runs. The rest of the club is 23-17 around him.

The ABL is the middle rung, so it is the only league here where you can fall and climb in the same breath: each conference winner gets a bye, second and third play in, both finalists of the American Championship are promoted to MLB, and the bottom three are relegated to the FBL. The Spiders share the Eastern Conference lead with the Royeaux; the bye is there for whoever blinks second. The drop is 5 games below them, where the Violets is the best of the clubs on relegation pace.

Through 40 games they were 23-17, scoring 239 runs and allowing 208 (+31). As of the May 13 export they are in first place in the Eastern Conference, and 2nd of 20 in the ABL. Runs scored and allowed like that typically buy about 23 wins in 40 games. They are 8-3 in one-run games and 0-2 in extra innings.

The hitters

Tino Martinez has 64 hits, 13 walks and 19 strikeouts in 171 trips; in 2001 he had a .830 OPS over 635 PA. Mark Sweeney has been the Spiders hitter opponents least want to see: .299/.377/.485, 4 homers, 21 driven in. Tino Martinez leads the club with 12 home runs. On the bases, Kerry Robinson is 19-for-25 stealing. As a team they are hitting .307/.357/.481, 6th of 20 in the ABL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 52 home runs. That 1.170 OPS is also the surprise. In 2001, per the export, Tino Martinez managed .830 over 635 plate appearances, which is a long way from here. The weakest regular bat has been Placido Polanco, at .269/.284/.339 in 176 plate appearances.

The pitchers

In the rotation, Ricky Bones has been the steadiest: 3-2 with a 3.21 ERA, 27 strikeouts and 17 walks in 42.0 innings. Jason Schmidt (4.02 ERA in 56.0 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 4.90 ERA, 9th of 20 in the ABL; starters 5.31, relievers 4.15. Closing has mostly meant Arthur Rhodes: 7 saves, 4.30 ERA. In the rotation, R.A. Dickey has a 7.19 ERA over 46.1 innings.

The Spiders have played eleven games decided by one run. They are 8-3 in them. Nobody is quite sure how much of that is skill, which is what makes it the most interesting thing about a 23-17 team.

The best and worst of it

The best night was May 5, a 17-4 win over the Coquis. The worst was Apr 14, a 10-2 loss to the Padres. Their best 10-game stretch went 8-2 (Apr 27 to May 6); their worst, 3-7 (Apr 19 to Apr 29).

The fine print is fun here. They once scored 17 runs in a game (May 5, against the Coquis); that one game is 7% of their season's runs.

What next

What comes next: 10 games starting May 14, 5 of them at home, against the Loons, the Pirates, the Reds, the Beavers, opponents with a combined 197-203 record (.492). Neither a gift nor an ambush. One more thing to watch: Tino Martinez is on the milestone watch, closing on 300 doubles.
