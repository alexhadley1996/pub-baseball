FBL · 2002-04-14 · By Walter Haines, special to Diamond Daily

Luis Polonia, Off the Bench

Ryan Vogelsong struck out 10 for Hartford, Gary Sheffield hit a three-run homer, and the Yard Goats took a 4-1 lead into the ninth. Then Santo Domingo's Luis Polonia, 38, came off the bench and hit a two-out, three-run walk-off homer against Curt Leskanic.

SANTO DOMINGO, Dominican Republic — Luis Polonia has a seat at the end of the Gallos’ bench, next to the water cooler, and he doesn’t like being moved out of it. He’s 38. He’s been in the big leagues, been out of them, played in Mexico, played winter ball here for longer than some of his teammates have been alive. He says he knows when he’ll be needed, and on Sunday afternoon he knew in the eighth inning.

“I told Kevin [Orie], ‘Get my bat ready,’” Polonia said. “He said, ‘We’re down three.’ I said, ‘Yeah. Get my bat ready.’”

In the bottom of the ninth, with two outs and two men on, Polonia pinch-hit and hit a three-run homer off Curt Leskanic. The Gallos beat the Hartford Yard Goats 5-4 in front of 26,284 people who, by the sound of it, will be talking about it for a long time.

Vogelsong’s day

For eight innings, this was Ryan Vogelsong’s game. The Hartford right-hander came in with a 5.54 ERA and an 0-1 record, and he struck out 10 Gallos in 7⅔ innings, gave up three hits and allowed one run, on Trot Nixon’s two-out double in the first.

“Best I’ve thrown in a long time,” Vogelsong said. “Maybe ever. I’ll feel good about it tomorrow.”

Hartford got all its runs off Cal Eldred, who struck out nine. Greg Myers homered leading off the second. In the third, Gary Sheffield, still playing shortstop and still hearing about it, hit a three-run homer to make it 4-1.

“I’ve been feeling it for about a week,” Sheffield said. “That was nice. And then we lost.”

The ninth

Luis Vizcaino finished the eighth for Hartford. Leskanic, who had saved two games this season, came on for the ninth.

He got Victor Martinez on a ground ball, then walked Trot Nixon. Kevin Orie doubled. Leskanic walked Ricky Ledee to load the bases, and Morgan Burkhart, pinch-hitting, lofted a fly ball that scored a run. 4-2, two outs, two on.

Manager Greg Anastasopoulos looked down the bench, and he didn’t hesitate.

“You know what Luis is?” Anastasopoulos said. “He’s a professional hitter. Doesn’t matter if it’s the first inning or the ninth or if he hasn’t swung all week. You put a bat in his hand, he’s ready.”

Polonia hit the second pitch he saw over the wall in right.

Afterward

There was a celebration on the field. There was a longer one in the stands. Kyle Farnsworth, who had pitched a scoreless ninth, got the win, his second.

Leskanic, in the visitors’ clubhouse, answered every question.

“I walked two guys,” he said. “You can’t walk two guys there. He hit a good pitch, I’ll say that. But I put them on.”

Santo Domingo is 9-4, one of the best records in the FBL. Hartford is 7-6. Vogelsong is 0-1 still.
