40-Game Check-In · ABL · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

The Athletics Have to Earn Every Run

New Orleans Athletics, 20-20 through 40 games

Imagine trying to win baseball games while hitting a home run roughly every 13 innings. The Athletics do not have to imagine it.

Through 40 games they were 20-20, scoring 231 runs and allowing 244 (-13). As of the May 13 export they are 3rd in the Eastern Conference, 3 games back, and 11th of 20 in the ABL. Runs scored and allowed like that typically buy about 19 wins in 40 games. They are 7-6 in one-run games and 3-2 in extra innings.

What is driving it

Their most productive hitter is Nick Swisher (1B, 21), at .285/.403/.514 with 7 HR. Raul Mondesi leads the club with 8 home runs. As a team they are hitting .286/.358/.419, 17th of 20 in the ABL by OPS through each club's first 40, with 27 home runs.

In the rotation, Kerry Wood has been the steadiest: 4-2 with a 4.75 ERA, 46 strikeouts and 23 walks in 55.0 innings. Aaron Myette (6.09 ERA in 34.0 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 5.89 ERA, 19th of 20 in the ABL; starters 6.27, relievers 5.40. Ray King has 10 saves and 2 blown.

The surprise might be Nick Swisher, who arrived without a 2001 top-tier line worth the name (under 150 plate appearances) and is hitting .285/.403/.514. The weakest regular bat has been Tim Bogar, at .200/.299/.313 in 136 plate appearances. In the rotation, Denny Lail has a 8.05 ERA over 34.2 innings.

A couple of small, true things. When they score three runs or fewer they are 3-8; when they score six or more, 14-5. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks. Bernie Williams has walked 21 times and struck out 15, a ratio that belongs to a different, more patient century.

The stakes

The ABL is the middle rung, so it is the only league here where you can fall and climb in the same breath: each conference winner gets a bye, second and third play in, both finalists of the American Championship are promoted to MLB, and the bottom three are relegated to the FBL. They sit 3rd in the Eastern Conference, 3 games back of the Spiders, inside the play-in places. The drop is 2 games below them, where the Violets is the best of the clubs on relegation pace.

The best night was Apr 10, a 19-8 win over the Gamblers. The worst was Apr 17, a 16-5 loss to the Loons. Their best 10-game stretch went 7-3 (Apr 9 to Apr 19); their worst, 4-6 (Apr 1 to Apr 10).

Looking ahead

The next stretch is 10 games (4 at home) against the Padres, the Violets, the Royeaux, the Angels. Those clubs are 213-187 combined, a .532 winning percentage, so the schedule is not doing them any favors. There is no luck to give back and none to collect: 20 wins, about 19 expected. What changes the picture from here is health and Nick Swisher, not arithmetic. A quarter of a season tells you what a team is capable of. The other three quarters tell you what it is. For the Athletics, the interesting part starts now.
