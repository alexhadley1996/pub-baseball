Roundup, April 3: Hartford comeback of the season

3 tiers (FBL, MLB, ABL), 20 games noted.

FBL Daily highlights

  • Hartford comeback of the season: down 9–0 after two, stormed back to win 11–10 on 16 hits. Box: SaSa 10, HYG 11
  • Richmond beat Orlando 10–2. Box: RiFS 10, ORLJ 2
  • Charlotte: Erik Milton threw a complete game; Louisville fell to 0–3. Box: ChKn 6, LoBa 1
  • Memphis over Carolina: Pablo Sánchez went 0-for-5 with 4 K; Erik Bedard was solid but wild. Box: CDT 4, MePh 10
  • Inland Empire 2, Oklahoma City 0: Omar Daal shut it down. Box: SB66 2, OKC 0
  • Monterrey and Santo Domingo stayed unbeaten: Gallos hit three homers; Jon Garland dominated in a 2–1.

MLB Daily highlights

  • Astros 8, Blue Jays 2: veteran Ron Gant led Houston. Box: TOR 2, HOU 8
  • Braves added Hideki Matsui on a one-year deal; he went 1-for-3 with 2 BB, but Atlanta fell to Miami. Box: MIA 8, ATL 2
  • Phillies 5, Rockies 1: big days from Horacio Estrada and Joe Crede. Box: PHI 5, COL 1
  • Seattle comeback win: Coco Crisp and Sean Burroughs each had three hits. Box: SEA 8, WCB 4 (10)
  • Cardinals stayed unbeaten: powered by a Khalil Greene HR and Jermaine Dye’s second. Box: STL 4, DAL 2
  • Brooklyn finally won: Derrick Lee starred again in a 6–3 win over Arizona. Box: BrGo 6, ARI 3
  • Dodgers dramatic win: four in the 9th, won in the 10th; Juan González and Jason Varitek went deep. Box: LAD 10, MIL 8 (10)
  • Yankees over A’s: Brian Tober pitched well; Bonds homered again. Box: NYY 6, OAK 4
  • Seals over Wolverines: late homers by Bengie Molina and Jimmy Rollins. Box: DET 4, SFS 5

ABL Daily highlights

  • Rick Ankiel masterpiece: Kansas City’s 22-year-old lefty went 7.2, allowing 1 hit and striking out 11 vs San Juan. Box: KCM 7, SJC 3
  • Cleveland walked it off: 4-run 9th capped by a Josh Phelps grand slam. Box: CLE 7, NASH 6
  • Montreal 9, New York Knights 5: Richard Hidalgo hit two 2-run HR. Box: MON 9, NYK 5
  • Portland 3–0: Salt Lake fell to 0–3. Box: PoBe 9, SLB 3
  • Anaheim 10, Las Vegas 3: Marquis Grissom doubled and hit a grand slam. Box: LAA 10, LVG 3
