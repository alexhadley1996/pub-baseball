40-Game Check-In · MLB · May 13, 2002 · By Staff

Griffins at 40: Small Ball, Big Questions

Dallas Griffins, 18-22 through 40 games

The Griffins have hit 25 home runs in 40 games, which is fewer than most of the clubs they play. They have to score some other way, every day, and they are 18-22 doing it.

Through 40 games they were 18-22, scoring 140 runs and allowing 166 (-26). With 41 games in the books as of May 13 they are 18-23, 8th in the West Division, 8 games back, and 16th of 20 in MLB. By the old Pythagorean arithmetic, that is a 17-win team so far. They are 4-5 in one-run games and 1-2 in extra innings.

What is driving it

Jose Mayobanex Fernandez has been the Griffins hitter opponents least want to see: .277/.379/.546, 9 homers, 33 driven in. Pokey Reese has 9 steals (caught 3 times). As a team they are hitting .235/.306/.348, 20th of 20 in MLB by OPS through each club's first 40, with 25 home runs.

Chris Haney leads the starters with a 2.45 ERA over 55.0 innings (30 K, 24 BB). Jesus Olague (4.30 ERA in 44.0 IP) has been the next most reliable. The staff as a whole has a 3.77 ERA, 6th of 20 in MLB; starters 4.09, relievers 3.17. Carlos Almanzar has 5 saves and 1 blown.

The surprise might be Jose Mayobanex Fernandez, who arrived without a 2001 top-tier line worth the name (under 150 plate appearances) and is hitting .277/.379/.546. Mike Mordecai has not looked like himself, or at least like his 2001 self: .727 OPS then, .525 now.

There was a stretch, Apr 30 to May 5, when the Griffins lost six straight. Seasons recover from that sort of thing all the time, but they do not forget it, and neither does the standings page.

The fine print is fun here. When they score three runs or fewer they are 5-19; when they score six or more, 8-1. Baseball is sometimes exactly as simple as it looks.

The stakes

MLB has no wild card and no consolation prize: division winners play for the title, the last-place club in each division goes down to the ABL. The Griffins are 8 games behind the Athletics in the West Division. The floor is 1 game below them, where the Dodgers currently stand on relegation pace.

The best night was Apr 23, a 7-0 win over the Brewers. The worst was Mar 31, a 13-3 loss to the Diamondbacks. Their best 10-game stretch went 8-2 (Apr 19 to Apr 29); their worst, 2-8 (Apr 30 to May 10).

Looking ahead

The next stretch is 10 games (5 at home) against the Yankees, the Athletics, the Cherry Blossoms, the Braves. Those clubs are 210-200 combined, a .512 winning percentage, so it will mostly be up to them. Their record is about what their runs say it should be, which is reassuring or alarming depending on which side of .500 you are standing on. Watch Jose Mayobanex Fernandez: the offense has run through him. None of this is settled, which is the point of May. But the Griffins have given us enough to argue about, and that is all you can ask of 40 games.
