Roundup, April 5: Marlins 5, Seals 4

3 tiers (MLB, ABL, FBL), 23 games noted. Lead: Marlins 5, Seals 4.

MLB Marlins 5, Seals 4 Box: SFS 4, MIA 5

Miami beat San Francisco after surrendering an early 4–0 lead, then winning on a Lee Stevens walk-off solo HR in the 9th. The bullpen duo of Dan Miceli and Kazuhiro Sasaki stayed strong again.

MLB Red Sox 10, Athletics 9 (14 innings) Box: OAK 9, BOS 10 (14)

Boston and Oakland played a 14-inning thriller. Boston rallied from 6–1 down, then again from 8–5 down in the 9th, storming back against Oakland closer Mariano Rivera. Kevin Young and David Bell each drove in three. Oakland had tagged Randy Johnson for 6 runs in 7 innings despite 11 Ks and 1 BB.
