Montreal Royaux and houston Astros have by far the worst systems. Inland Empire's is deep but without impact talent as is Washington's; Chicago's is also deep but a little better, Minnesota's, Austin's and Honolulu's are as well. memphis has some real talent coming up, making them a real team for the future as their current lineup is already great by FBL standards. Tampa looks like likely to be relegated but they've got a superb system, with 2 in the top 5 and 5 in the top 50. Baltimore, like Montreal, also looks a strong promotion/future side with a good deep system of 5 top 50 prospects. Dallas' pitching prospects are iincredible and Atlanta has two potential stars in the ABL next year in Mark Prior and Robinson Cano. The relegation candidates in the MLB look like the best systems as well, with only the Cubs and Red Sox looking half decent. The Rockies have lots of deep talent with 5 guys in the top 40.
