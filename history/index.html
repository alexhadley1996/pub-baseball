History

Every season the export knows about: standings, league leaders, award winners and All-Stars for 1871–2001 from real MLB history, plus 2002 in this universe. Each of the league's 30 original franchises also has a History tab on its team page.

Seasons before 2002 are real MLB history from the OOTP export, mapped by OOTP onto this league's 30 original franchises (club names are as they were in that season). 2002 is this universe. Player names are exactly as the export stores them: OOTP gives some historical players generated names (the 714-homer career is filed under “Sanchez Ruth”), and the site keeps them. Only players active in 2002 have player pages, so older names are plain text.
