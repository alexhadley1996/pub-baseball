League impact summary Alexander Hadley, May 12

Biggest Season-Changers

  • Adam Everett (Dallas) and Damian Moss (Inland Empire) are effectively lost for the year — both teams must reshuffle defensive and pitching identities.
  • Noah Lowry’s shoulder injury is catastrophic for Baltimore’s rotation depth and likely forces an early trade or innings gamble.
  • Eric Young and Bernie Williams both going down for New Orleans strips speed, defense, and table-setting from an already thin offense.

Teams Hit Repeatedly

  • New Orleans (ABL): Multiple outfield injuries plus pitching attrition.
  • Honolulu (FBL): Middle infield + center field instability at once.
  • Philadelphia (MLB): Catching injuries stacking up early.

Quiet Winners

  • Miami (MLB) has largely avoided catastrophic injuries — one more reason their early success feels sustainable.
  • San Francisco (MLB) loses Ruben Sierra, but depth and defense cushion the blow.
  • Portland (ABL) dodges major injuries despite poor performance — trade leverage remains intact.

Early Warning Signs

  • Hamstring and shoulder injuries are clustering league-wide.
  • Several young pitchers (Lowry, Moss, Carrara) already showing durability red flags.
